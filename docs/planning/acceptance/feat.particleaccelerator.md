## This file's role (and the relationship to FEAT-055)

FEAT-055 (`feat.megafacilities`) owns the **shared expert-workforce gate** (numeric threshold on `engine.education`'s research-points output — ASM-512) and names "SpaceX/CERN/Aldermaston-class science/defence mega-facilities" as a catalogue anchor. **This file does not re-specify that gate** (GR#3). It owns the accelerator's *facility-specific* behaviour: the massive electricity/water draw into the utility network, the research spillover into education and health, the FDI anchor, and prestige. The boundary is the same "gate shape vs facility mechanics" split `feat.megafacilities.md` draws against `engine.education.md`. Two BOW-pointer corrections are load-bearing and logged: the draw routes through `engine.consumption`'s `UtilityAPI`, **not** `engine.fuel` (ASM-714), and "research spillover into health" routes through `engine.wellbeing` since `engine.health` is not a registered module (ASM-712).

## North-star vetting (dev-team-process.md v1.12 BA mandate)

- **Q4 (snowball) / Q5 (long bet).** The accelerator is the research flavour of the education snowball's summit — it *closes the loop* by feeding research back into education and health (the same output the gate reads), the spec's exemplar long bet made buildable.
- **Q1/Q2 (conflicting demand / felt no).** Enormous capex + land + a permit + day-one decommission liability, **plus** a permanent, massive electricity/water load that competes with every other consumer on the grid (a felt no in grid-capacity terms, not just money). The gate means the money is only spendable at all if education was funded first.
- **Q3 (consequence sticks).** The grid draw is permanent while the facility runs; the decommission liability never leaves the books until restoration.

## User stories

- **US-1.** As a player deep into the end-game whose education pipeline is funded, I need the accelerator gated on a large highly-trained scientific workforce that money/milestones/development-points cannot substitute for, so "money alone cannot buy it" is mechanical (§8; FEAT-055 AC-4).
- **US-2.** As the city's power/water network (`engine.consumption`'s `UtilityAPI`), I need the accelerator to post a massive, peak-load-aware electricity and water draw that stacks with every other load and is conserved through the network solve, so the facility is a real grid stressor, not a cosmetic demand note (BOW "massive electricity/water draw coupling to the grid").
- **US-3.** As the research economy (`engine.education`'s research-points output), I need the accelerator to amplify the city's research rate, so "research rate ××" (§MP) is a real, queryable spillover into the same output that gates the end-game, not a static badge.

- **US-5.** As `engine.fdi` (the anchor), I need the accelerator to draw an FDI anchor prospect, so "FDI anchor" (BOW desc) is a real draw, not narration.

- **US-7.** As `data.catalogue`/`buildings.json`, I need the accelerator to extend/detail the existing `hadron_research_ring` mega-project entry rather than fork the supplement.
