## 1. Why this item exists

Aaron's brief (epic `FEAT-2326609792`, verbatim): "every vehicle needs somewhere to park" and vehicles "need fuel, including EV charge points." inc0 already tabulated the rates (`parking.json`, `vehicle_classes.json`) and inc0's scale ladder already carries CITY-WIDE totals (`parkingSpacesDemanded` etc.) for reference, but nothing today attaches those rates to the ACTUAL built city the way inc2's `demandForecastOf` attaches trip rates to actual residents/workers — a city built with more office than the ladder's assumed shape has more parking demand than the ladder total says, and today nothing sees that. This increment does for parking/fuel/EV exactly what inc2 did for trips: per-tile, bottom-up demand from what is actually built, checked directionally (not force-reconciled — §2) against the ladder's own aggregate.

## 2. The design in one paragraph

**Demand is bottom-up, not ladder-redistributed (Decision-free — matches inc2's own precedent).** `scale_ladder.json`'s `parkingSpacesDemanded`/`fuelLitresDemandedPerDay`/`evKWhDemandedPerDay` are POPULATION-rung aggregates computed at inc0 table-build time from a hypothetical average city shape; `demandForecastOf`'s `TileDemand[]` reflects the ACTUAL built city (real occupancy, real land-use mix). Exactly as inc2 never rescales `personTrips` to match a ladder total, this increment computes parking/fuel/EV demand bottom-up from `TileDemand` and treats the ladder fields as a DIRECTIONAL sanity bound in tests only (§3 ACs), never a reconciliation target — two independently-sourced numbers are allowed to diverge; forcing them equal would be a second, competing model of the same quantity (GR#3 violation in the other direction). **Parking (AC-1/AC-2/AC-3).** Per demand-generating tile, `SPECS[b.spec].kind` classifies the land use into `parking.json`'s `demandByLandUse` categories (`residential`→`densityTier(sp)` 1→`dwelling_low_density`, 2/3→`dwelling_high_density`, applied to `residentsActual`; `office`/`retail`/`industrial`→their per-job rate, applied to `workersActual`); `kerbVsOffStreet.byDensityBand[ladderPointOf(s).densityBand]` splits the tile's demand into kerb/off-street shares. Kerb SUPPLY comes from `roads.json` `classes[].parking===true` on orthogonally-adjacent road tiles, converted from frontage metres (`traffic.json webconsoleMetresPerTile`) to a space count via a NEW `parking.json` field `kerbSpaceLengthMetres` this increment's build must add under `data/traffic/parking.json` first (ASM-1524) — never a hand-typed metre figure in TypeScript (GR#15). Off-street supply is **fixed at 0** (Decision D1 — no placeable parking-structure spec exists, ASM-1525). **Fuel + EV (AC-4/AC-5).** Per-tile vehicle-km by class: passenger classes (`car`/`motorbike`/`taxi`) from `TileDemand.personTrips × modeShareOf(point)[mode] ÷ occupancyForMode(mode)` (the SAME conversion `assignedFlowOf` already performs, reused not re-derived — GR#3) `× avgTripLengthKm`; freight classes (`cargo_van`/`rigid_truck`/`articulated_truck`) from `TileDemand.freightVehicleTrips × freightTonnesByVehicleClass` share `× avgTripLengthKm`, mirroring inc2's `blendedFreightVehicleCapacity` apportionment pattern per-class instead of blended. Each class's vehicle-km splits into liquid-fuel litres and EV kWh via `vehicle_classes.json`'s `fuelLitresPerKm`/`kWhPerKm` and `fuel.json eras[0]` ("early") `car/van/truckEVShare` (Decision D2 — webconsole has no era/time concept yet, ASM-1526; motorbike/taxi ride the `car` EV share, no separate mode in `fuel.json`). EV charge-point DEMAND reads `scale_ladder.json`'s `evChargePointsNeeded` directly (no bottom-up rate table exists for this one figure — the ladder IS the data source here, not a cross-check); supply is fixed at 0 (Decision D1 again). **Shortfalls (AC-6).** Every demand/supply pair exports a bounded `[0,1]` factor (`0` = fully met, `1` = zero supply against non-zero demand), city-wide and per-tile for parking, city-wide only for fuel/EV charge points (no per-tile fuel-station/charge-point location exists to attach a per-tile figure to, honestly — a per-tile figure would fabricate a location this increment does not model). **No coupling (AC-7).** These are read-only diagnostic exports; inc9 (rewards) or a future increment is where a shortfall could feed happiness — explicitly out of scope here, mirroring inc3 AC-8/inc4 AC-7.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

**AC-1 — `parkingDemandOf(s): Map<tileKey, {demanded: number; kerbSpaces: number; offStreetSpaces: number}>` — land-use-classified, density-band kerb/off-street split.**
Per `demandForecastOf(s)` tile, classify `SPECS[b.spec].kind` into a `parking.json demandByLandUse` key (residential via `densityTier`, office/retail/industrial via their per-job rate) and apply the rate to `residentsActual`/`workersActual`; split via `kerbVsOffStreet.byDensityBand[ladderPointOf(s).densityBand]`. A tile whose kind has no matching `demandByLandUse` entry (e.g. `power`, `park`) contributes **0**, never `NaN` or a fallback rate.
**Check:** a fixture asserting non-zero `residentsActual`/`workersActual` up front (precondition-first, per the inc3 BUG-862 lesson), then one low-density-tier dwelling tile and one high-density-tier dwelling tile with EQUAL `residentsActual` must produce DIFFERENT `demanded` (1.5 vs 0.6 spaces/dwelling) — a same-rate mutant cannot distinguish tiers.
**False-pass note:** a fixture using only one density tier cannot catch a "single flat rate for all residential" mutant.
**Mutant:** apply `demandByLandUse.dwelling_low_density` to every residential tile regardless of `densityTier` — reds the two-tier fixture.

**AC-2 — `kerbParkingSupplyOf(s): Map<tileKey, number>` — kerb spaces from adjacent parking-eligible road tiles only, using a data-sourced space length.**
For every demand tile, sum orthogonally-adjacent road tiles whose `roads.json classes[].parking === true`; spaces = (adjacent parking-eligible tile count `× webconsoleMetresPerTile`) `÷ parking.json.kerbVsOffStreet.kerbSpaceLengthMetres` (new field, ASM-1524 — build must add it to `data/traffic/parking.json` with source/year/confidence before this AC is implemented; **not** a literal in TypeScript).
**Check:** a fixture with one demand tile adjacent to a `residential_street` (parking:true) tile and a second, otherwise-identical fixture where that road is replaced with `avenue_2_plus_2` (parking:false) — the first has `kerbSpaces > 0`, the second has exactly `0`.
**False-pass note:** a fixture using only parking-eligible roads cannot catch a mutant that ignores the `parking` flag entirely (counts every adjacent road tile).
**Mutant:** count every adjacent road tile regardless of its class's `parking` flag — reds the `avenue_2_plus_2` fixture (produces `>0` instead of `0`).

**AC-3 — `parkingShortfallOf(s): {cityShare: number; perTile: Map<tileKey, number>}` — bounded `[0,1]`, off-street supply honestly fixed at 0 (Decision D1).**
`perTile.get(k) = demand > 0 ? clamp(1 - (kerbSupply + 0) / demand, 0, 1) : 0` (off-street supply is the literal `0` per D1/ASM-1525, not omitted — a future placeable-parking increment changes this term, not this one). `cityShare` = population-weighted city-wide version of the same formula (residents+workers weight, mirroring inc4 AC-4's weighting basis).
**Check:** a tile with `kerbSupply >= demand` asserts `shortfall === 0` (never negative); a tile with `kerbSupply = 0, demand > 0` asserts `shortfall === 1` exactly.
**False-pass note:** a fixture that only checks "shortfall is between 0 and 1" cannot catch an inverted formula (`supply/demand` instead of `1 - supply/demand`) — both ranges overlap `[0,1]`; the two exact-boundary cases above are required.
**Mutant:** invert the formula to `clamp(supply/demand, 0, 1)` — reds the `kerbSupply=0` case (produces `0` instead of `1`).

**AC-4 — `vehicleKmByClassOf(s): Record<'car'|'motorbike'|'taxi'|'cargo_van'|'rigid_truck'|'articulated_truck', number>` — city-wide vehicle-km/day by class, bottom-up from actual demand.**
Passenger classes: Σ over tiles of `personTrips × modeShareOf(point)[mode] ÷ occupancyForMode(mode) × avgTripLengthKm`. Freight classes: Σ over tiles of `freightVehicleTrips × numericFieldOrZero(point, 'freightTonnesByVehicleClass.'+id) ÷ totalShare × avgTripLengthKm` (same normalisation `blendedFreightVehicleCapacity` uses, per-class instead of blended). `occupancyForMode` exported additively from `trafficAssignment.ts` (currently private, `trafficAssignment.ts:192`).
**Check:** a fixture asserting non-zero `personTrips`/`freightVehicleTrips` up front, then doubling ONE tile's `residentsActual` (holding others fixed) must change ONLY `car`/`motorbike`/`taxi`/`bus`-linked totals proportionally, never the freight classes — proving the two derivations are independent, not cross-contaminated.
**False-pass note:** a fixture that only checks "totals are positive" cannot catch a mutant that sums passenger AND freight into the same class bucket.
**Mutant:** accumulate freight vehicle-km into the `car` bucket instead of `cargo_van`/`rigid_truck`/`articulated_truck` — reds the independence fixture (car total changes when only freight demand changes).

**AC-5 — `fuelAndEVDemandOf(s): {litresPerDay: number; evKWhPerDay: number; byClass: Record<string,{litres:number; kwh:number}>}` — city-wide, era-fixed EV split, directional cross-check against the ladder.**
Per class: `litres = vehicleKm × fuelLitresPerKm × (1 - evShare)`, `kwh = vehicleKm × kWhPerKm × evShare` (`kWhPerKm===null` classes, e.g. `rigid_truck`/`articulated_truck`, contribute `kwh=0` — honest absence, never a fabricated conversion). `evShare` reads `data/fuel.json eras` by **id** `'early'` (Decision D2/ASM-1526 — `.find(e => e.era==='early')`, never `eras[0]` by index), `car`→`carEVShare` (motorbike/taxi ride the same figure, no separate `fuel.json` entry), `cargo_van`→`vanEVShare`, `rigid_truck`/`articulated_truck`→`truckEVShare`. A missing `'early'` entry or missing EV-share field is a registry error (`MET-Vxxx`, claimed via `add-error.js claim-range`), never a hand-typed fallback.
**Check:** a fixture computes `litresPerDay`/`evKWhPerDay` from a known `TileDemand` fixture and asserts the result is the SAME ORDER OF MAGNITUDE (within 10x either direction) as `numericField(ladderPointOf(s), 'fuelLitresDemandedPerDay')`/`'evKWhDemandedPerDay'` for a fixture built to roughly match that rung's assumed shape — directional, per §2, never an exact-match assertion (the two are independently-sourced models).
**False-pass note:** an exact-match assertion against the ladder total would be a FALSE requirement (§2 explains the two are deliberately independent) and would also silently pass a mutant that just COPIES the ladder field instead of computing bottom-up — the order-of-magnitude check plus a SEPARATE fixture proving the bottom-up figure changes when `TileDemand` changes (and the ladder figure does not) together prove real computation happened.
**Mutant:** return `numericField(ladderPointOf(s), 'fuelLitresDemandedPerDay')` directly instead of computing from `vehicleKmByClassOf` — passes the order-of-magnitude check but reds the "changes when TileDemand changes, ladder field does not" fixture.

**AC-6 — `evChargePointShortfallOf(s): number` — ladder-sourced demand (no bottom-up rate exists), supply fixed at 0 (Decision D1).**
`demand = numericField(ladderPointOf(s), 'evChargePointsNeeded')` (the ladder IS the data source for this one figure, per §2 — no per-charge-point throughput table exists to derive it bottom-up). `shortfall = demand > 0 ? 1 : 0` (supply is the literal `0` per ASM-1525; a non-zero supply term is a future placeable-EV-charger increment's job).
**Check:** a fixture at a rung with `evChargePointsNeeded > 0` asserts `shortfall === 1`; a fixture at a rung with `evChargePointsNeeded === 0` (the lowest rungs, verified against the actual table, not assumed) asserts `shortfall === 0`.
**False-pass note:** a fixture using only a high-population rung cannot prove the zero-demand branch is reachable and correct, not just accidentally always 1.
**Mutant:** hard-code `shortfall = 1` unconditionally — reds the zero-demand-rung fixture.

**AC-7 — no wellbeing/money coupling (mirrors inc3 AC-8 / inc4 AC-7); overlay explicitly out of scope this increment.**
No file this increment touches writes to `s.budget`/`s.treasury`/any `*Pounds`/`*Revenue`/`*Cost` field, or any wellbeing-named identifier (`s.happiness`, `wellbeingOf`, `commuteWeight`). No `MapView.tsx` change this increment — the epic's own §2 note calls the overlay "optional" and `FEAT-2326609792`'s increment list reserves UI work for inc10; adding an ad-hoc overlay here would pre-empt that item's own round/verdict.
**Check:** `grep -n "budget\|treasury\|Pounds\|Revenue\|happiness\|wellbeing\|commuteWeight" webconsole/src/sim/parkingFuel.ts` returns no matches; `git diff --stat` for this increment's commit touches no file under `webconsole/src/components/`.
**Mutant:** wire `parkingShortfallOf`'s `cityShare` into any happiness/wellbeing-named identifier — caught the moment such an identifier appears in the new module.

**AC-8 — data discipline (GR#15), determinism (GR#21), registry errors, structural cost bound.**
No hand-typed parking rate, kerb-space length, EV share, or fuel/kWh-per-km figure in TypeScript — all read from the in-tree mirror. All exports `memoOnState`; no `Date.now`/`Math.random`/`localStorage`; byte-identical across repeated calls and a StrictMode double-mount (the inc2 BUG-756 class). Cost bound: one O(tiles) pass for parking demand/supply/shortfall, one O(tiles) pass for vehicle-km, O(1) lookups for fuel/EV split and charge-point shortfall — never O(citizens), never a second segment-graph traversal (this increment does not need `segmentAdjacencyOf`).
**Check:** `grep -n "Date.now\|Math.random\|localStorage" webconsole/src/sim/parkingFuel.ts` (excluding tests) returns no matches; a 10-rerun determinism test (JSON-identical); a structural pin asserting the module never imports `trafficAssignment.ts`'s Dijkstra/adjacency exports.
**Mutant:** hand-type a `kerbSpaceLengthMetres` fallback (e.g. `?? 5.5`) instead of reading the new `parking.json` field — reds AC-2's fixture the moment the field is edited to a different value in a mutation test (the hand-typed fallback ignores the edit).

## 5. Decisions for Aaron (recommend, never block)

- **D1 — placeable parking/fuel/EV specs vs derived-only supply.** Recommend **derived-only** this increment (AC-2/AC-3/AC-6 as specified: real kerb supply from roads, off-street/EV-charger supply fixed at 0) — a placeable parking structure / fuel forecourt / EV-charger spec is a catalogue change (new `SPECS` entries, sprite, cost, upkeep) that belongs in its own follow-up item once the demand side is proven, not smuggled into a "parking, fuel and EV" data-plumbing increment.
- **D2 — EV-share era mapping.** Recommend fixing `'early'` now (near-zero EV, conservative/fail-closed) with a Q for Aaron: should webconsole game time later map to a `fuel.json` era (needs a webconsole era/tick-count concept that does not exist today), or should EV share instead track the scale-ladder rung (population) directly — a genuinely different model from MOD-062's time-based eras and would need its own assumption.
- **D3 — power-model wiring.** `fuelAndEVDemandOf(s).evKWhPerDay` is a genuine electricity demand (GR#3) that `powerStats(s)` (`data.ts:3944`) does not yet see. Recommend this increment ships `fuelAndEVDemandOf` as a diagnostic-only export (converting kWh/day to an MW figure is a one-line follow-on) and defers the actual `powerStats` wiring to a small follow-up BOW item scoped to `data.ts`'s power-need term specifically, rather than reopening that heavily-commented core function inside a "parking, fuel and EV" increment's own round.
