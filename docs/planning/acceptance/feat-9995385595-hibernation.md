## Purpose

Trap 1 of the compute-offload review: an open tab holds a WebSocket, the host never scales to zero, and one forgotten tab costs about GBP 125 a month on a 10M replica (6x the cap). Today only a city with ZERO connections is unloaded (`evictIdle`, 5 min). Nothing unloads a city whose tab is open but nobody is playing. This item adds that: a per-city idle timer (15 min with no accepted player command), a tab-hidden disconnect in the browser (5 min), and the host-level step that lets a paid host scale to zero once nothing is loaded.

## Rulings applied (Aaron, 2026-11-17)

- **OQ-1 RESOLVED.** A connected city sleeps after 15 min with no player command. There is NO warning toast and no "stay awake" control (AC-1, AC-45).
- **OQ-4 RESOLVED.** Store unreachable at hibernate: retry with back-off; after 3 failed attempts unload anyway (the journal is durable and replays on wake). Stay loaded and alert only if the JOURNAL itself is failing (AC-20, AC-19/21, AC-48).
- **OQ-5 RESOLVED.** Scale to zero: the process exits itself about 2 min after nothing is loaded and nothing is connected; Spot and Azure host classes only, never pix (AC-25, AC-27).
- **OQ-6 RESOLVED.** Click to wake, never wake on tab focus (AC-37).
- **Lead defaults (not Aaron-ruled):** OQ-2 notify first, then quiesce, snapshot, close (AC-10, needs a pre-close hook in `stopBy`; the waking doc's AC-H1 must be aligned). OQ-3 keep the single 20 s snapshot budget until inc2 measures 10M/30M (AC-16). OQ-7 no keep-alive opt-out (AC-40). OQ-8 no hibernate rate limit in v1; log cycle counts (AC-30).

## Definitions

- **Two idle clocks (both exist, both unload through the one BUG-1197 stop path `runningCity.stopBy`; they differ in trigger, in client notification and in what they cost the player):**

  | | Evict (existing, FEAT-1972079942) | Hibernate (new, this item) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Applies to | a city with `active == 0` connections | a city with `active >= 1` connections |
  | Clock | `cityEntry.idleSince`, set when `active` drops to 0; threshold `IdleEvictTimeout` = 5 min (strictly greater than, checked each `evictSweepInterval`) | last engine-accepted player command, threshold 15 min (new, per cityEntry, in cmd/metroserve) |
  | Trigger code | `CityHost.evictIdle` (calls `d.e.city.stop()` per doomed city, serially) | new `hibernateIdle` sweep (same map-removal plus `stopping` marker pattern, calls `stopBy` with a shared deadline, in parallel) |
  | Client told | nobody connected, so nobody | `server.hibernating` then close 1000 `hibernate` (AC-10) |
  | Snapshot | BUG-1197 final snapshot via `stop()` | the same, via `stopBy` |

  A city can satisfy at most one clock at a time (`active == 0` versus `active >= 1`); a hidden tab that closes its socket at 5 min changes the city from the hibernate clock to the evict clock (AC-39). Both removals happen under `CityHost.mu`, so whichever sweep reaches a city first owns it and the other finds it absent (AC-46).
- **Evict (existing):** unchanged by this item except that it shares the stop path (AC-14) and is the path a hidden-tab disconnect ends in.
- **Hibernate (new):** the server decides a city is not being played, tells its connected clients, closes their sockets with code 1000 reason `hibernate`, and unloads the city through the same stop path as evict. The city is simply not loaded; its durable state is the final snapshot plus the journal. There is no third persisted state and no "hibernated" flag on disk (ASM-H1).
- **Idle (for hibernation):** a city with at least one connection whose **last engine-accepted player command** is more than 15 min old. Tick advance, server events, subscribe/camera queries and client keep-alives do not count as activity (ASM-H2). A sim that has been paused is idle by the same rule (pausing is itself an accepted command; later accepted commands, including building while paused, reset the clock; see ASM-H7).
- **Host-all-idle:** the CityHost holds no city entry (ready or building), has no open connection, and no city is mid-stop (the meter's "not active" condition, feat-9995385596 Definitions, plus the stopping markers).
- **Clean wake:** a wake whose newest committed snapshot covers the journal head (empty tail), as defined in feat-9995385594 section G. A hibernate normally produces one; a skipped snapshot (AC-18) does not.

## A. Triggers: per-city idle

- **AC-1** *(amended: toast removed)* Given a loaded city with one connection and no accepted command for 15 min of wall clock (running sim), When the hibernation sweep runs, Then the city is hibernated (AC-9 to AC-14). The client receives no frame of any kind before `server.hibernating` (no warning, no countdown, no toast; AC-45).
- **AC-2** *(amended: now a case of AC-1)* Given a loaded city whose sim was paused by an accepted command and no further accepted command arrives for 15 min with the tab open, When the sweep runs, Then the city is hibernated even if non-command traffic (keep-alives, subscribe, camera queries) continued. The test uses a paused sim as one of AC-1's fixtures; there is no separate pause timer (ASM-H7).
- **AC-3** Given a command is accepted at minute 14, When 15 min of idle are re-measured, Then the idle clock restarted at the command; the city is not hibernated at minute 15.
- **AC-4** *(amended)* Given the sim is paused at minute 10 and un-paused at minute 12 (both accepted commands), When the sweep runs at minute 15, Then the city is not hibernated (the idle clock restarted at minute 12).
- **AC-5** Given a rejected or malformed command (validation error, wrong build, unauthorised, `E-HIBERNATING`), When it arrives, Then it does NOT reset the idle clock (only commands the engine accepted count). A client spamming bad commands cannot hold a city open. The tracker is updated where the accepted result is produced (the command loop's result path, `snapshotCommandSource.SendResult` in cmd/metroserve), not where frames arrive.
- **AC-6** Given two connections to one city (reconnect overlap, tests; BUG-1196 normally refuses a second), When either sends an accepted command, Then the clock resets for the city; hibernation closes every connection to the city.
- **AC-7** Given a city still building or waking (cityEntry not ready, zero `idleSince`), When the sweep runs, Then the city is never hibernated (same invariant as `evictIdle`).
- **AC-8** The idle threshold (15 min) and sweep interval are configuration with the Q100157 defaults, not constants; both are launcher flags and env vars alongside `--shutdown-snapshot-deadline`, with the registry code `E-HIBERNATE-CONFIG` on an invalid value. The clock source is injectable so tests run on a fake clock with no sleeps (never wall-clock bounds in CI).

## B. The hibernate sequence (one stop path)

Code fact the sequence is built on: `stopBy` today runs `cancel -> join tick driver -> join command loop -> join pump -> transport.Close -> writeFinalSnapshot -> comp.Close`. Once the pump is joined no frame can be relayed to a client, so a `server.hibernating` notification must be sent BEFORE `rc.cancel()`.

- **AC-9** Given AC-1 fires, When hibernation starts, Then the city is first removed from the host map and a `stopping` marker installed in the same critical section under `CityHost.mu` (the `evictIdle`/`Shutdown` pattern, BUG-764 F1), so a concurrent GetOrCreate for the key waits for the unload and then rebuilds; it never builds a second engine on the same paging claim. The sweep waits on `entry.ready` before stopping, as `evictIdle` does.
- **AC-10** *(rewritten to the OQ-2 default)* Order for a connected client: (1) pre-check (AC-48): the journal is healthy, otherwise the city stays loaded and nothing is sent; (2) the city is marked hibernating, after which new commands are rejected with `E-HIBERNATING` (AC-23) and `server.hibernating {reason: "idle"}` is sent to every connection with the `lifecycle.hibernating` capability (feat-9995385597 AC-P3), via a new **pre-close notify hook invoked in `stopBy` before `rc.cancel()`**; (3) `stopBy` quiesces at a tick boundary (an in-progress tick is never aborted; cf. meter AC-30), closes the transport so each socket closes code 1000 reason `hibernate` (AC-H2 behaviour for clients without the capability), then attempts the final snapshot (AC-14, AC-16, AC-20); (4) the composition is closed and the paging claim released. The notification is delivered before the socket closes and before the snapshot starts, so the player sees "saving" rather than a silent freeze. Sending is best-effort (AC-11) and has its own short bound so a slow peer cannot delay the unload. Test: a recording fake asserts the order notify, cancel, joins, transport close, snapshot, `comp.Close`. **Open code detail for dev:** whether the 1000/`hibernate` code and reason can be carried from `stopBy` through `transport.Close` into wsserver's socket close is not established by this review; if it cannot, the close reason is a wsserver change and belongs to BUG-1209's fix or this item, not to the client doc.
- **AC-11** Given the notification fails to send (peer gone), When hibernation continues, Then the failure is logged once with the city key and does not abort or delay the unload.
- **AC-12** Given hibernation closes a connection, When the pump returns, Then the session-gate claim (BUG-1196) is released exactly once (feat-9995385597 AC-D6), so the same user clicking wake immediately is not refused with MET-P043.
- **AC-13** Given BUG-1209 (a city unload leaves the socket open), When hibernate unloads a city, Then the socket is actually closed and the host's connection count drops to zero; a test with a real WS client asserts the client observes close 1000 `hibernate` and `conns == 0` within 2 s of the stop path returning. This AC cannot pass until BUG-1209 is fixed; BUG-1209 is a BOW dependency (ASM-H3).
- **AC-14** Hibernate, evict, SIGTERM and meter-stop all unload through the single `runningCity.stopBy` path of BUG-1197. No second snapshot-and-stop implementation is added. The only additions are (a) the pre-close notify hook, (b) a caller-supplied reason, (c) the hibernate-only retry wrapper of AC-20 and (d) the result return of AC-47. A guard test (source-level) asserts there is exactly one call site of `Composition.FinalSnapshot` in cmd/metroserve (today: `writeFinalSnapshot` in snapshotdriver.go).
- **AC-15** Hibernate and evict pass `abandonOnDeadline = false` (the process keeps running; the snapshot goroutine is awaited before `comp.Close`); only the process-exit path passes true. A test with a slow-snapshot fake asserts a hibernated city is not Closed under a still-running build.
- **AC-16** The final snapshot uses the existing deadline (`DefaultShutdownSnapshotDeadline`, 20 s, flag `--shutdown-snapshot-deadline`), per attempt. Hibernation does not introduce a second, longer budget (OQ-3 default: revisit after inc2 measures 10M and 30M).
- **AC-17** *(amended: code fact)* Given hibernation of N cities in the same sweep, When they unload, Then they run in parallel under one shared absolute deadline (the `CityHost.Close` pattern: `stopBy(deadline, false)` per city in goroutines), so N cities never take N times the budget. Code fact: `evictIdle` today calls `stop()` serially, and `stop()` computes `time.Now()+finalBudget` per city, so N evicted cities take up to N x 20 s. The new sweep must not copy that; moving `evictIdle` to the same parallel pattern is recommended in the same change and is covered by a test with two slow fakes.

## C. Failure cases

- **AC-18** *(amended)* Given `FinalSnapshotResult.SkipReason` is `tick-zero`, `trailing-command`, `partial-tick` or `deadline`, When hibernation completes, Then the city IS still unloaded (the previous snapshot plus the journal restore it exactly, BUG-1197), the skip reason is logged with the city key and tick, the next wake replays the journal tail and is NOT counted as a clean wake, and `/health` increments the per-reason skip counter (AC-29). Hibernation never stays loaded merely because a snapshot was skipped. The journal-related skip reasons `journal-dirty` and `persist-halted` are handled by AC-48 before the sequence starts, and by AC-19 if they first appear after it has started.
- **AC-19** *(rewritten to the OQ-4 ruling)* Given `JournalStatus()` shows the engine halted on a journal failure (BUG-472) or the journaler dirty at the pre-check (AC-48), When the idle timer fires, Then the city stays loaded, no client is told, and an ERROR-level line carrying the halted state and its correlation id is logged (rate-limited to once per 15 min per city) with the `hibernateBlockedJournal` counter incremented; the sweep re-checks each interval and proceeds as soon as the journal is healthy. If the journal fails only AFTER the sequence has started (race between pre-check and snapshot), the city is already notified and quiesced, so it unloads, the ERROR line is logged and the skip is counted; there is nothing further to protect.
- **AC-20** *(rewritten to the OQ-4 ruling)* Given the final snapshot attempt returns a genuine error (store unreachable, `PutSnapshot` failing; today reported as MET-P062 and never returned to the caller, hence AC-47), When the reason is `idle`, Then the hibernate wrapper retries up to 3 attempts in total, each under its own 20 s deadline, with a configurable back-off between attempts (default 5 s), and after the third failure the city unloads anyway (the journal is durable and replays on wake), logged once as a degraded hibernate (`hibernateDegraded` counter, `E-HIBERNATE-DEGRADED`). Retries run while the city is quiesced under its `stopping` marker (ASM-H6): a snapshot needs a quiescent engine, so "stay loaded and retry" is not possible after `stopBy` has cancelled the city; clients were told to sleep before the retries began, and a wake click queues behind the marker (AC-22). Worst case before unload is about 3 x 20 s plus 2 back-offs. The meter-stop and host-drain paths make one attempt only (meter AC-30: a retry loop keeps billing past budget; SIGTERM has a hard grace period). Test with a store that fails N times for N = 1, 2, 3, 4 and asserts attempts made, final unload, counters and that no attempt runs after unload.
- **AC-21** *(merged into AC-19/AC-48; kept as a pointer)* Journal-unreachable behaviour is specified in AC-19 and AC-48.
- **AC-22** Given a client reconnects while the city is mid-hibernate (including during AC-20 retries), When the handshake arrives, Then it is accepted and waits behind the `stopping` marker (feat-9995385597 AC-H5, AC-W `queued`); it is never refused as busy and never attached to the dying city.
- **AC-23** *(amended)* Given a command arrives after the city is marked hibernating (AC-10 step 2), When it is received, Then it is rejected with `E-HIBERNATING` and the client keeps its local intent; it is never accepted-and-lost. A command already accepted before the mark is journaled and is in the final snapshot or in the journal tail. Test: send commands before the mark, between mark and cancel, and between pump join and transport close, and assert each is in the replayed state or rejected, never acknowledged and absent.
- **AC-24** *(amended order)* Given the process is killed (SIGKILL, Spot loss without grace) at each step of AC-10 and inside each AC-20 retry, When it restarts, Then restore succeeds from the latest durable snapshot plus the journal (the previous snapshot stays latest because the write is temp-then-rename), and the restored digest equals an uninterrupted run (AC-32). One test per step boundary, using the BUG-1197 fault hooks.

## D. Host-level: scale to zero

- **AC-25** *(amended to OQ-5 ruling)* Given a Spot or Azure host class and Host-all-idle holding continuously for `--scale-to-zero-after` (default 2 min; flag and env), When the timer fires, Then the process runs its normal SIGTERM shutdown path (feat-9995385597 AC-D1, which finds nothing loaded) and exits 0 by itself, independent of any platform scale-to-zero setting. A new connection or GetOrCreate before the timer fires cancels it; once shutdown has begun, new handshakes are refused with the retryable `E-SERVER-DRAINING` (AC-D1 step 1) and the platform or launcher restarts the process on demand (cold start is outside this item). The timer is armed from the host's own state, not from a `/health` probe, so a health poller cannot defeat it. Injectable clock and injectable exit function in tests.
- **AC-26** Exit-for-scale-to-zero does not double snapshot: every city was already unloaded by hibernate or evict, so the exit path finds no loaded city. A test asserts zero `FinalSnapshot` calls on the exit path in that case.
- **AC-27** *(amended)* Given the pix host class or a dev host, When all cities hibernate, Then the process stays up and idle and the scale-to-zero timer is never armed (a test asserts class `pix` and `dev` never arm it, whatever the flag says). With no city loaded, Go heap in use after `runtime.GC()` is below a stated multiple of the empty-host baseline for a 1M-citizen city (measured in inc1; never an RSS or wall-clock assertion).
- **AC-28** *(amended: reasons)* The meter's `exhausted` stop (feat-9995385596 AC-30) and the launcher's Spot-reclaim SIGTERM use the same `stopBy` path but are NOT hibernations: their client notice is `server.draining` (reason `budget` per meter AC-30, `deploy`/`shutdown` per sibling AC-P2), and they make one snapshot attempt (AC-20). `server.hibernating` is sent only for idle hibernation (and `reason: "operator"` if an operator command is added; out of scope). Hibernation does not depend on the meter or the launcher existing. Once this item lands, the meter's stop should call the shared path with its own reason (small follow-up recorded on FEAT-9995385596).

## E. Observability

- **AC-29** `/health` gains, additively (existing fields untouched, no wire break): per city `idleSeconds` (since last accepted command) and `paused` boolean; host-level counters `hibernations`, `hibernateSnapshotSkips` (by skip reason), `hibernateDegraded`, `hibernateBlockedJournal`, `hibernateCycles` (per city key count of hibernate then wake cycles, OQ-8). All reads are cheap atomic loads; /health never blocks on the engine or store (existing contract).
- **AC-30** Every hibernate logs one structured line with city key, reason, idle seconds, snapshot attempts and final result (id, wrote, skip reason, tick), snapshot duration, whether connections were open and the city's hibernate cycle count. Logs contain no player data. No rate limit on hibernate cycles in v1 (OQ-8 default).
- **AC-31** *(amended: reuse)* New error codes are proposed and registered only through the add-error tool by the lead. Proposed (not registered): `E-HIBERNATING` (command rejected during hibernate, AC-23), `E-HIBERNATE-DEGRADED` (unloaded without a snapshot after retries), `E-HIBERNATE-CONFIG` (invalid flag), `E-HIBERNATE-JOURNAL` (blocked by a failing journal, AC-19). The snapshot write failure itself reuses the existing MET-P062 (and skips MET-P061); the draft's `E-HIBERNATE-STORE` is dropped as a duplicate.

## F. Determinism

- **AC-32** Given a city run to tick T uninterrupted, and the same city hibernated at a tick boundary and woken (clean wake), then run to T, When the engine digests are compared at T, Then they are byte-identical. Repeat for a skipped-snapshot hibernate (journal replay wake), for a degraded hibernate (AC-20) and for 1, 7 and 30 consecutive hibernate/wake cycles. Hibernate timing is wall-clock and is never an input to the simulation: nothing about when or whether a city hibernated appears in any hashed state.
- **AC-33** *(amended)* The idle clock, sweep timer and scale-to-zero timer read the wall clock only in cmd/metroserve and never inside internal/engine. Test: the existing wall-clock-in-engine gate stays green and a source check asserts no file under internal/engine gains a new `time.Now` or timer use from this item.
- **AC-34** Hibernating a paused city and waking it leaves the sim paused (pause state is part of the persisted world, or the client re-asserts it; whichever it is today is recorded in the test and not changed here).

## G. Client: Page Visibility disconnect

- **AC-35** Given the page becomes `hidden` (Page Visibility API) for 5 min continuously, When the timer fires, Then the client closes its WebSocket with code 1000 and enters the `asleep` state shown as "City asleep - click to wake" (same state as feat-9995385597 AC-H3), and does NOT auto-reconnect.
- **AC-36** Given the page returns to `visible` before 5 min, When it does, Then the timer is cancelled and the connection is untouched. A hidden-then-visible-then-hidden sequence restarts the 5 min.
- **AC-37** *(OQ-6 RESOLVED)* Given the page becomes visible again after the visibility disconnect (or after a server hibernate), When the user focuses the tab, Then the client does not reconnect on its own; only a deliberate click on the asleep badge wakes. A test fires `visibilitychange` to visible and asserts no connect attempt.
- **AC-38** The 5 min timer uses a monotonic source (`performance.now`) and tolerates browser timer throttling in background tabs (Chrome clamps background timers to 1 s and, after 5 min, intensive throttling to 1 per minute): the disconnect may fire up to 60 s late but never early. Test with the injectable clock and a simulated throttled tick.
- **AC-39** *(amended: sweep slack stated)* The visibility disconnect is a close the server sees as a normal 1000; the server-side result is the evict clock (`active == 0` for more than 5 min, AC-14's shared stop path), so the combined worst case for a forgotten hidden tab is about 5 min + up to 60 s throttle + 5 min + one `evictSweepInterval`, roughly 10 to 12 min, not 15 min + 5 min. A forgotten VISIBLE tab is hibernated at 15 min (+ one hibernate sweep interval).
- **AC-40** *(OQ-7 default: rewritten)* There is no keep-alive opt-out and no player setting that disables the visibility disconnect or the server idle timer; a second-monitor watcher gets the 15 min server limit. A test asserts the client has no such configuration path. (The draft's "off when the only visible window" clause is dropped: it was not observable.)

## H. Wake and meter interaction

- **AC-41** Wake after hibernate is the feat-9995385597 waking flow with no hibernate-specific path: a clean wake on a 10M city meets the Q100153 p95 15 s target measured by feat-9995385594 AC-P3, not by this item.
- **AC-42** *(amended)* Hibernated time is not active (feat-9995385596 AC-36/AC-5): the meter stops counting within one sample interval (1 s) of the last city unloading. Snapshot time before the unload counts (meter AC-37). Test with the fake clock across a full hibernate then wake cycle and assert the counter rose by (idle + snapshot) seconds to within one sample interval before the unload and by zero (within one sample interval) between unload and the next claim. (The draft said "exactly"; the meter samples in whole seconds.)
- **AC-43** Given the meter state is `exhausted`, When a hibernated city's user clicks wake, Then the wake is refused by the meter, not by hibernation (feat-9995385596 AC-30/AC-31 and its refusal wording `E-METER-BUDGET`); the client stays `asleep` with the meter's message.
- **AC-44** The 30 s meter flush and the hourly in-play snapshot (A2Bev001 v23.4, inc3) both complete or cancel cleanly if a hibernate begins; neither writes after the city is unloaded (an in-play save that finishes after unload is discarded like a post-deadline final snapshot).

## I. Added by review

- **AC-45 (no warning, OQ-1).** Given a city approaching 15 min idle, When the sweep runs at any time before the threshold, Then no server frame, log line visible to the client or client UI change occurs (no toast at minute 14, no "stay awake" control); the first signal is `server.hibernating`. A test records every frame to a connection across minutes 0 to 15 and asserts only normal traffic precedes the hibernate frame.
- **AC-46 (evict versus hibernate race).** Given `evictIdle` and the hibernate sweep reach the same key at the same instant (the last client disconnects as the 15 min threshold passes), When both run, Then exactly one stops the city (the loser finds the key absent under `CityHost.mu`), `FinalSnapshot` is called once, and no connection is attached to a stopping city. Race-detector test with a hook (like `onEvictStopping`) landing the other path inside the window.
- **AC-47 (stop path returns its result).** Given `writeFinalSnapshot` today returns only a completion channel and logs the outcome, When AC-18, AC-20, AC-29 and AC-30 need the result, Then the snapshot driver returns the `FinalSnapshotResult` and error (as a value or via a callback) to `stopBy` and its caller without changing what is written or logged for evict, SIGTERM and Close. Regression test: existing BUG-1197 behaviour and log lines unchanged.
- **AC-48 (journal pre-check).** Given the hibernate threshold passes, When the sweep considers a city, Then it reads `comp.JournalStatus()` (cheap, no engine lock beyond its existing contract) before marking or notifying anything; `PersistHalted` or a dirty journaler means the city stays loaded and AC-19 applies. Test with a halted engine and a dirty journaler fake: no frame sent, city stays in the map, counter and ERROR line present, later recovery proceeds to hibernate.

## Assumptions (proposed, not registered)

- **ASM-H1** There is no persisted "hibernated" flag: a hibernated city is indistinguishable on disk from an evicted or cleanly stopped one.
- **ASM-H2** "No command" means no engine-accepted player command; ticks, keep-alives and queries do not count. The activity tracker lives in cmd/metroserve (per-cityEntry), not in the engine.
- **ASM-H3** BUG-1209 is fixed before or with this item (it is a BOW dependency). Hibernation is the first feature whose normal path unloads a city that still has an open socket.
- **ASM-H4** The wake path (feat-9995385597) and the meter (feat-9995385596) land independently; this item must pass with either absent (stubs for the notification and meter hooks).
- **ASM-H5** Host-level exit is acceptable on Spot and Azure because the platform or launcher restarts on demand; on pix the process stays up.
- **ASM-H6 (lead to confirm, Aaron's ruling implemented as the nearest feasible design).** Aaron's "retry with back-off, stay loaded" for a failing store cannot be literal after quiesce, since a snapshot needs a quiescent engine and `stopBy` has already cancelled the city. The retries therefore run inside the stop, under the `stopping` marker, after clients were told; the player-visible effect is the same (the city is gone after at most about 70 s) and the journal is never at risk. If Aaron wants the city to remain playable during retries, the alternative is a store-health pre-probe before notify (cheap write-and-delete test object) that defers hibernation while the store is down, for at most 3 sweeps; this review recommends the in-stop design as simpler.
- **ASM-H7** "Paused" from Q100157's "15 min no command or paused" is covered by the no-command clock: pausing is an accepted command, and a player who builds while paused is active. A paused city with commands flowing is not hibernated. Aaron's ruling states only "no player command".
