## 2. Acceptance criteria

**AC-1 (a Domain adapter drives the REAL Go citizens engine).** Given a new `PopulationDomain` in `internal/converge/population_domain.go` implementing `Domain` (`Name()` returns `"population"`), when `Run(journal)` executes then it constructs a fresh instance of the real `engine.citizens` surface, seeds the cohort from the journal's `seed` ops, advances via the real `AdvanceDayTick` / `AdvanceMonth` (`registry.go:1402/1613`), and samples `births`, `deaths`, `population` (via `TotalPopulation`) at each sample tick; no field is computed locally from literals. *Mutation:* replace the engine read with `population = seed + k*ticks` locally -> `TestPopulationDomain_EngineReadsPinned` (modelled on `services_domain_test.go:435 TestServicesDomain_EngineReadsPinnedAtTick90`) reddens. *Surface:* the Sample values in the trajectory and the report table.

**AC-2 (determinism, GR#21).** Given the same Journal run twice against two fresh instances, then the two Trajectories are `reflect.DeepEqual`, and run with `-count=3`. *Mutation:* introduce a map-range-dependent ordering inside the adapter (e.g. ranging a map of cohorts to seed) -> reddens. Mirrors `TestServicesDomain_DeterministicTrajectory` (`services_domain_test.go:53`).

**AC-3 (the Contract is explicit, tiered, and covers every sampled field).** Given `Contract()` then: `population`, `births`, `deaths` are `TierDistribution` with a named `Window` (suggest 12 samples = one simulated year at monthly sampling, placeholder) and a named `BandPct` constant in `tolerance.go` (not inline; precedent `ServicesCoverageEpsilon`); the starting seed headcount (a sanity row) is `TierExact`. A sampled field with no Contract entry fails closed (`codeUnknownTolerance`, existing behaviour), proven by `TestPopulationDomain_ContractCoversEverySampledField` (precedent `services_domain_test.go:220`). Band values are **placeholders** for Aaron's balance pass, with a directional test only (balance number regime): a candidate with 2x the birth rate must fail, a candidate equal to ref must pass. *Mutation:* widen `BandPct` to 100% -> the "2x births must fail" test reddens.

**AC-4 (TierDistribution is exercised for real, including its warm-up guard).** Given a 36-month journal when compared then no divergence is reported for ticks with fewer than `Window` samples accumulated (early warm-up never misreported), and at least one tick past warm-up is actually evaluated (assert evaluated-count > 0, otherwise a vacuous pass). *Mutation:* set `Window` larger than the journal -> zero evaluated ticks -> the "evaluated > 0" assertion reddens (a gate that can't evaluate must not report success, verification standards).

**AC-5 (TS candidate fixture is emitted, never hand-authored).** Given a new emitter `webconsole/test/converge-fixture-emit-population.mjs` (pattern: `converge-fixture-emit.mjs` and `-services.mjs`), when it runs a headless reducer from a documented seed city for the same journal then it writes `internal/converge/testdata/population-webconsole-v1.json` in exactly the `fixtureFile` shape (`fixture.go:35-65`: `{"domain":"population","samples":[{tick,values}]}`), with a **no-silent-drift test** in node-test (precedent: the BUG-978/BUG-1089 reddens - a TS change that moves the trajectory without a regenerated fixture fails CI). Migration (move-in/out) is disabled or journalled identically on both sides. *Mutation:* change `BIRTH_RATE_PER_TICK` in `engine.ts:488` -> the drift test reddens until the fixture is regenerated; the regeneration is the lead's act, not the developer's.

**AC-6 (report-only divergence is recorded, not hidden).** Given `population_ab_test.go` (precedent `finance_ab_test.go:243 TestFinanceAB_KnownDivergence_NonEmpty` and `:277 ..._GreenIfFixturesMatch`) when Compare runs Go-ref vs TS-candidate then (a) the known divergence is asserted non-empty with the exact fields listed (so a silent convergence or a silent new divergence both fail loudly and force a human to update the report), (b) a matching-fixture case passes, (c) a deliberately divergent fixture fails (the gate has teeth, precedent `services_domain_test.go:114`).
