## User stories

- **US-1.** As the player, I need the game's single tax authority to withhold PAYE income tax and NI from every working netizen's earnings each month — so wage income is taxed at source, the way the UK does it, and I see the net flow, not a lumped "taxes" total.
- **US-2.** As the player, I need a simple corporation tax on netibusiness profits, stamp duty on freehold/leasehold purchases only (renters moving pay nothing — Aaron's ruling), and monthly council tax per household — so each tax is a legible, independent flow with its own instrument and its own revenue line.
- **US-3.** As the player, I need import/export duty on international movements only (chunnel, RoRo ferry, air freight), with zero duty on commuting and domestic out-of-tile goods — so cross-border trade is taxed, local movement is not, and the distinction follows real goods-movement classification, not a flat rate on everything.
- **US-4.** As `engine.fiscal` and `ui.screen.finance`, I need every HMRC collection posted through `engine.finance`'s ledger under the owning instrument's revenue line — so the fiscal circuit and the finance screen see real transactions, never a second tax total the collection engine keeps for itself.
- **US-5.** As the player, I need every HMRC rate to be a data-file instrument I can set from the existing tax panel (the `engine.tax` `TaxAPI` surface) — so the tax authority is controlled by the same panel as every other instrument, not a hardcoded separate set of sliders.

## Scope

The monthly HMRC collection engine: PAYE + NI withholding from working netizens' earnings, corporation tax on netibusiness profits, stamp duty on tenure purchases (freehold/leasehold purchases only), monthly council tax per household, import/export duty on international movements (chunnel/RoRo/air freight only) — each computed from live engine state, posted through `engine.finance`'s ledger, with all rates loaded from `data/tax_instruments.json` (extended with NI + stampDuty instruments). It does **not** re-implement the tax panel, the rate-setting, or finance bookkeeping — it consumes `engine.tax`'s instruments, `engine.citizens`/`engine.firms`/`engine.households`/`engine.freight` state, and posts through `engine.finance`. The land-registry annual audit is a FEAT-202 dependency (Escalation 2), not part of this build.

## Acceptance criteria

### Functional — the collection engine

- **AC-1 (US-1; PAYE+NI withholding at source, per working netizen).** Each month, every working netizen's earnings are assessed against the `paye` (and new `ni`) instruments and withheld before the net wage is paid; the gross→net split and the withheld amount are each attributable per netizen (or per cohort with a documented aggregation — no single lumped "payroll taxes" figure hiding the two instruments). Check: a passing test drives a known working netizen cohort with a known wage and asserts (a) the withheld PAYE amount, (b) the withheld NI amount, (c) the net paid, each separately and each posting a distinct revenue line (`grep -rn "func Test.*PAYE\|func Test.*N I\|func Test.*[Ww]ithhold" internal/engine/hmrc/*_test.go` or the dispatch-resolved package). **False-pass risk:** a single "payroll tax" bucket that internally splits but posts one line — assert two distinct revenue lines.
- **AC-2 (US-2; corp tax on netibusiness profits, simple).** Corporation tax is assessed on `engine.firms` netibusiness profits each month using the `corporationTax` instrument (existing in `data/tax_instruments.json`). Check: a passing test drives firms with known profits and losses and asserts (a) profit months produce the assessed amount, (b) loss months assess zero (no negative tax / no carry-forward unless the proposal specifies one — flag as an Escalation if it does), (c) the revenue posts through `engine.finance` (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Cc]orp.*[Tt]ax\|func Test.*[Ff]irms.*[Tt]ax" internal/engine/hmrc/*_test.go`). **False-pass risk:** corp tax computed on revenue not profit — assert the base is net profit (firms' profit, not turnover).
- **AC-3 (US-2/US-3; stamp duty on purchases ONLY, council tax monthly, import/export international-only).** Three instruments, three distinct rules: (a) stamp duty fires on freehold/leasehold **purchases** only — a renters-moving event pays nothing (Aaron's ruling); (b) council tax is assessed monthly **per household** using the `councilTax` instrument; (c) import/export duty fires on **international movements only** (chunnel, RoRo ferry, air freight) — commuting and domestic out-of-tile goods pay zero. Check: three passing tests, one per rule, each asserting the taxed/untaxed split and the distinct revenue line (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Ss]tamp\|func Test.*[Cc]ouncil\|func Test.*[Ii]mportDuty\|func Test.*[Ee]xportDuty" internal/engine/hmrc/*_test.go`). **False-pass risk:** stamp duty or import duty firing on a non-triggering event (a rent renewal, a domestic delivery) — assert the negative case explicitly for each.

### Data and registry

- **AC-4 (US-5; all rates data-file instruments extending tax_instruments.json, FEAT-056 loader precedent).** Every HMRC rate is an instrument in `data/tax_instruments.json` loaded via the FEAT-056 loader precedent (the `foundation.data` path `engine.tax` already uses) — `paye`/`corporationTax`/`councilTax`/`importDuties` extend their existing entries, `ni` + `stampDuty` are NEW instruments added to the file. No HMRC rate is a hardcoded Go literal. Check: `grep -n "\"ni\"\|\"stampDuty\"\|\"paye\"\|\"councilTax\"" data/tax_instruments.json` matches; `grep -rnE "[0-9]+(\.[0-9]+)?%|[0-9]+p" internal/engine/hmrc/*.go` (excluding `_test.go`) finds no bare rate literal (rates come from the loaded data) (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Ll]oadInstrument\|func Test.*[Hh]mrc.*[Rr]ate" internal/engine/hmrc/*_test.go`). **False-pass risk:** a rate hardcoded "temporarily" that the data file shadows — assert the engine reads the loaded value (mutate the file, assert the engine follows).
- **AC-5 (US-4; collections post through engine.finance's ledger, never a second ledger).** Every HMRC collection posts through `engine.finance` under the owning instrument's revenue line (the same `engine.finance` outbound `engine.tax` already has) — the collection engine keeps no parallel money total. Check: a passing test asserts each instrument's collected amount appears in `engine.finance`'s ledger under the matching instrument id, and the finance conservation invariant holds across a collection month (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Ll]edger.*[Hh]mrc\|func Test.*[Cc]onserv.*[Tt]ax" internal/engine/hmrc/*_test.go`). **False-pass risk:** a collection that computes the amount but never posts it (silent ledger zero) — assert the ledger line, not the computed value.

## Escalations

- **For Bev — mkey + collection edges (blocks dispatch, GR#25).** Register `feat.hmrc` in master-plan → regenerate code.json. The collection engine needs consumer/outbound edges that do NOT exist today: `engine.citizens` (PAYE/NI — currently nothing in `engine.tax`'s consumer set reads citizens), `engine.firms` (corp), `engine.households` (council), `engine.freight`/`engine.logistics` (import/export classification), and the `engine.finance` posting edge. Whether this is a new `internal/engine/hmrc/` module or a collection layer folded into `engine.tax` (which is MOD-052 DONE) is the lead's call (Escalation 3).
- **For Aaron/Bev — stamp duty + the land registry (FEAT-202 dependency).** FEAT-204's Desc says stamp duty on freehold/leasehold **purchases** and "the land registry annual audit is an HMRC function" — but **NO land-registry module is registered in code.json** and FEAT-202 (Land registry: crown/freehold/leasehold tenure ledger + annual consistency audit) is a SEPARATE P1 item LATER in the queue. Two options: (a) scope stamp duty to the purchase *event* (a price × rate charge on a purchase transaction from `engine.build`/`engine.market`) without a tenure ledger, leaving the annual audit to FEAT-202; or (b) gate FEAT-204's stamp-duty AC on FEAT-202 landing first. Recommend (a) — stamp duty is a transaction tax, the audit is a separate registry concern; Aaron to confirm.
- **For Bill/Bev — module split.** `engine.tax` is MOD-052 DONE with rate-setting + revenue query surfaces; folding a monthly *collection engine* into it risks touching a done module. Recommend a distinct `feat.hmrc` collection engine that consumes `engine.tax`'s instruments + `engine.finance` for posting, leaving MOD-052's surfaces untouched. Route to the lead before dispatch.
- **Balance regime.** Every HMRC rate (PAYE bands, NI, corp rate, stamp rates, council band values, import/export duty rates) is a player-felt number — AC-1..AC-5 assert STRUCTURE (withholding happens, distinct lines post, negative cases are zero, rates load from data), never absolute values. Ship the rates as data-file placeholders (the existing FEAT-056 file + NI/stampDuty entries) with disclosure comments + a delegated balance proposal for Aaron.
- **Dependency risk.** `engine.tax` (MOD-052 DONE), `engine.citizens`, `engine.firms`, `engine.households`, `engine.freight`/`engine.logistics`, `engine.finance` are all registered and built on origin/main. FEAT-202 (land registry) is a separate later item — this build must not wait on it if Escalation 2's option (a) is approved.
