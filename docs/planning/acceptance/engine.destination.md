# Acceptance criteria — engine.destination (MOD-061)

**BOW code:** MOD-061
**Spec refs:** §48 Destination Leisure & Retail (`docs/METROPOLIS-MASTER-v2.1.md` lines 644-648, in full: two buildable regional-draw archetypes sharing §44 machinery — "Forest holiday resort (\"Center Parcs\")": 100+ ha woodland (BDI synergy: "the resort *wants* your nature"), lodges, subtropical pool dome ("energy hog"), ~1.5k jobs, "steady year-round staying visitors; countryside blight-free if screened"; "Mega-mall (\"Bluewater\")": unlocks as a pit-reclamation option (§32), "walls hiding it from the viewshed (blight-clever, historically exact)", 300+ shops' worth of retail floorspace, 7k jobs, "colossal parking (§38)", "bus/rail links required", "daily stock logistics in hundreds of tonnes"; effect: "hoovers regional retail spend *in*... while pressuring your own high streets (§35/§41 tension at maximum scale) — the town-centre-vs-mall war, player-refereed"); §32 Mining, Extraction & the Blight Model (line 460: the general blight/reclamation model — "Exhausted pits **reclaim**: lake (leisure/BDI+), country park, or landfill void" — the mega-mall's specific reclamation route; the noise-radius/viewshed blight mechanic mega-mall's "walls hiding it from the viewshed" deliberately defeats); §38 Parking (the registered `engine.destination`→`engine.parking` edge covering "colossal parking"); §35/§41 (the town-centre-vs-mall tension the spec cites by number for BOTH sections — high-street retail and café-strip vitality); Catalogue (line 1308: "Center Parcs & Bluewater archetypes | §48"); code.json `engine.destination` entry (inbound `DestinationAPI`, pattern "regional-draw machinery shared with TourismAPI"; **zero registered consumers**; outbound calls to `engine.tourism`, `engine.mining`, `engine.parking`).

## User stories

- **US-1.** As the player, I need two distinct buildable archetypes (forest resort, mega-mall) each with the spec's named characteristics (job counts, footprint, energy/parking demands), not one generic "destination" building with cosmetic variants.
- **US-2.** As the mega-mall specifically, I need my site eligibility gated on an exhausted-quarry reclamation state from `engine.mining`'s blight model (§32, the registered edge), so "Bluewater" is genuinely a pit-reclamation option and not buildable on arbitrary land.
- **US-3.** As the mega-mall, I need my regional-draw score to reuse `engine.tourism`'s decomposed portfolio-scoring machinery (the registered edge, per code.json's own "shared with TourismAPI" pattern and ASM-326's documented boundary), not a duplicated scoring formula (GR#3).
- **US-4.** As parking demand ("colossal parking, §38"), I need a real, queryable parking-capacity requirement sourced through the registered `engine.destination`→`engine.parking` edge.
- **US-5.** As the town-centre-vs-mall war (§35/§41), I need the mega-mall's regional spend-capture to measurably pressure high-street retail and café vitality — blocked pending BUG-058 findings #11/#12 (no `engine.destination`→`engine.shopping`/`engine.cafe` edges).
- **US-6.** As "bus/rail links required" (§48, literal text), I need mega-mall operation gated on transit connectivity via the registered `engine.destination`→`engine.traffic` edge — blocked pending BUG-058 finding #10.
- **US-7.** As "daily stock logistics in hundreds of tonnes" (§48), I need a real freight-demand figure sourced through `engine.logistics` — blocked pending BUG-058 finding #14.
- **US-8.** As the forest resort, I need BDI synergy — the resort's draw or effectiveness responding positively to surrounding biodiversity — a literal reading of "the resort *wants* your nature" (§48), not decorative flavour text.

## Scope

Two buildable destination archetypes (forest resort, mega-mall) and their named characteristics; mega-mall reclamation-site eligibility via `engine.mining`; regional-draw scoring reused from `engine.tourism`; parking-demand generation via `engine.parking`; BDI synergy for the forest resort. No construction/build-queue mechanics itself (`engine.build`, pending edge), no high-street/café pressure computation (`engine.shopping`/`engine.cafe`, pending edges), no transit-gating or freight-logistics mechanics themselves (`engine.traffic`/`engine.logistics`, pending edges).

## Acceptance criteria

### Functional

- **AC-1 (GR#20; code.json inbound contract).** A `DestinationAPI` exists matching code.json's registered pattern "regional-draw machinery shared with TourismAPI" — the regional-draw computation is delegated to (or built directly atop) `TourismAPI`'s decomposed portfolio-score machinery via the registered `engine.destination`→`engine.tourism` edge, not a parallel scoring implementation. Check: `go doc ./internal/engine/destination DestinationAPI` shows a regional-draw accessor; `grep -n "engine/tourism" internal/engine/destination/*.go` (excluding `_test.go`) finds a real import/call; `grep -rn "func Test.*[Ss]haredDrawMachinery\|func Test.*[Nn]oDuplicateScoring" internal/engine/destination/*_test.go` finds a test asserting the destination draw call actually reaches `TourismAPI` (via a fake satisfying the interface) rather than computing its own independent formula. A lazy-but-plausible violation: a destination-local reimplementation of the portfolio-score product that happens to look similar — this check rejects it by requiring the call to visibly reach the shared interface, not merely produce a plausible number.
- **AC-2 (§48 two distinct archetypes, named characteristics).** Forest resort and mega-mall are two distinct exported archetype types/constants, each carrying the spec's named figures as data-driven fields (not hardcoded): resort = ~1.5k jobs, 100+ ha footprint minimum, year-round staying-visitor draw shape; mega-mall = 7k jobs, 300+ shop-equivalent floorspace minimum. Check: `go doc ./internal/engine/destination` shows two distinct archetype identifiers with distinguishable field values; a passing test asserts the two archetypes' job-count and footprint fields differ and match the documented order of magnitude (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Aa]rchetypeDistinct" internal/engine/destination/*_test.go`); `grep -nE "1500|7000|1\.5|300" internal/engine/destination/*.go` (excluding `_test.go`) finds these figures sourced from loaded data, not bare literals (GR#15).
- **AC-3 (§48/§32 mega-mall reclamation-site eligibility — real gate, not cosmetic).** Mega-mall placement is legal only on a cell/site `engine.mining`'s blight model (the registered `engine.destination`→`engine.mining` edge) reports as an exhausted, reclaimable pit — attempting to place a mega-mall on a non-reclamation site is rejected. Check: `grep -n "engine/mining" internal/engine/destination/*.go` (excluding `_test.go`) finds a real import/call; a passing test attempts mega-mall placement on a fixture site `engine.mining` reports as NOT exhausted (via a fake) and asserts rejection, then on a site reported as exhausted and asserts acceptance (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Rr]eclamationGate\|func Test.*[Mm]egaMallSiteEligibility" internal/engine/destination/*_test.go`). A lazy-but-plausible violation: a mega-mall buildable anywhere with a cosmetic "reclaimed quarry" skin — this check rejects it by forcing the negative case and demanding rejection.
- **AC-4 (§48 mega-mall viewshed screening — "blight-clever" as a real effect).** A screened mega-mall (screening/walling applied) produces a measurably lower viewshed-blight contribution to neighbouring cells than an unscreened one, reusing `engine.world`/`engine.mining`'s general blight-model viewshed mechanic (§32) rather than a mega-mall-specific exemption from it. Check: a passing test compares a screened and unscreened mega-mall's viewshed-blight output on identical neighbouring cells and asserts the screened case is strictly lower (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Vv]iewshedScreening" internal/engine/destination/*_test.go`).
- **AC-5 (§48 forest resort BDI synergy — "the resort wants your nature", literal).** The forest resort's draw score or effectiveness responds positively to the surrounding region's Biodiversity Index (BDI), queried via whatever edge exposes BDI (documented — see Escalations, since no `engine.destination`→`engine.farming`/BDI-source edge is registered today and this AC's data source is therefore itself flagged pending confirmation). Check: a passing test raises the surrounding BDI figure (via a fake/fixture) and asserts the resort's draw-score or effectiveness term rises (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Bb]DISynergy" internal/engine/destination/*_test.go`). A lazy-but-plausible violation: a resort whose draw is entirely independent of any BDI input — rejected because the check demands the term actually move.
- **AC-6 (§48 "bus/rail links required" — BLOCKED, see BUG-058 finding #10).** Mega-mall operation (full job/spend-capture effect) is gated on measured transit connectivity via a registered `engine.destination`→`engine.traffic` edge — a mega-mall with no bus/rail link operates at a documented reduced/zero capacity, not full capacity. **This AC is blocked**: no such edge is registered today. Check (once unblocked): `grep -n "engine/traffic" internal/engine/destination/*.go` (excluding `_test.go`) finds a real import; a passing test removes transit connectivity (via a fake) and asserts operating capacity falls (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Tt]ransitGate" internal/engine/destination/*_test.go`).
- **AC-7 (§48 town-centre-vs-mall pressure — BLOCKED, see BUG-058 findings #11/#12).** Realised mega-mall regional-spend-capture measurably reduces a queryable retail-survival input to `engine.shopping` and/or the vitality input to `engine.cafe` (the "§35/§41 tension" the spec cites by both numbers). **This AC is blocked**: no `engine.destination`→`engine.shopping` or `engine.destination`→`engine.cafe` edge is registered today. Check (once unblocked): a passing test raises mega-mall regional spend-capture and asserts a measurable negative effect reaches `engine.shopping`'s or `engine.cafe`'s interface (via a fake) (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Tt]ownCentrePressure\|func Test.*[Mm]allWar" internal/engine/destination/*_test.go`).
- **AC-8 (§48 daily stock logistics — BLOCKED, see BUG-058 finding #14).** Mega-mall operation generates a daily freight-tonnage demand queryable by `engine.logistics`. **This AC is blocked**: no `engine.destination`→`engine.logistics` edge is registered today (BA-Registry's prior weak finding, upgraded to confirmed by this file's explicit-spec-text read). Check (once unblocked): a passing test asserts a non-zero, capacity-consuming freight-tonnage figure is queryable per operating mega-mall (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Ff]reightDemand" internal/engine/destination/*_test.go`).
- **AC-9 (§38 colossal parking demand — registered edge, not blocked).** Mega-mall operation generates a parking-demand figure queryable/consumable via the registered `engine.destination`→`engine.parking` edge, scaled to the spec's "colossal" characterisation (materially larger than a comparable retail-park-scale demand figure, if such a comparator exists in `engine.parking`'s own fixtures — otherwise checked structurally as "non-trivial and configurable", not against an invented absolute number). Check: `grep -n "engine/parking" internal/engine/destination/*.go` (excluding `_test.go`) finds a real import/call; a passing test asserts a non-zero parking-demand figure is pushed to `engine.parking`'s interface (via a fake) when a mega-mall is placed (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Pp]arkingDemand" internal/engine/destination/*_test.go`).

### Error handling

- **AC-10 (GR#7).** Placing an archetype on an invalid/oversized-for-footprint site, or querying an unregistered destination ID, returns a registry-sourced error (new `MET-E`-range code claimed at build time, per the README's Sprint-1 convention — no `engine.destination` sub-range pre-exists), never a panic or silent no-op placement. Check: `grep -n "MET-" internal/engine/destination/*.go` finds a registry code reference; passing test coverage (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Ii]nvalidSite\|func Test.*[Uu]nknownDestination" internal/engine/destination/*_test.go`).
- **AC-11 (GR#7).** A malformed or schema-invalid archetype-config data file (missing job count, negative footprint) produces a registry-sourced error at load time, never a silent default. Check: `grep -n "MET-" internal/engine/destination/*.go` finds a load-time code distinct from AC-10's; passing test coverage (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Mm]alformedArchetypeConfig" internal/engine/destination/*_test.go`).

### Determinism & safety

- **AC-12 (GR#20 outbound contract discipline).** `internal/engine/destination/*.go` imports no `engine.traffic`, `engine.shopping`, `engine.cafe`, or `engine.logistics` package while BUG-058 findings #10/#11/#12/#14 remain open. Check: `grep -rln "\"Metropolis/internal/engine/\(traffic\|shopping\|cafe\|logistics\)\"" internal/engine/destination/*.go` (excluding `_test.go`) finds **no match** while the findings are open.

Filed as comments on BUG-058, 2026-08-11 (findings #12/#14 upgrade BA-Registry's earlier "weak, single mention" logistics finding to confirmed, since this file's read treats the sentence as explicit spec text, not inference). Recommend the same resolution path already accepted for `engine.build`→`engine.season`: these are all consumer-side reads of already-live registered modules (`engine.traffic`/`engine.shopping`/`engine.logistics` all exist), so wiring carries no reverse-dependency risk.
- **BDI-synergy source edge unresolved (blocks part of AC-5).** §48 says the forest resort "wants your nature" (BDI synergy) but no module owns a registered BDI-query edge to `engine.destination` today, and BDI itself is owned by `engine.farming`/environment systems per §31 — outside this file's five-module scope to resolve. AC-5's check is therefore written against a documented fixture input rather than a named live edge; flagging for Bill/whichever BA owns `engine.farming`'s BDI export to confirm the right edge before this AC can be un-blocked in spirit (it is not formally BUG-058-blocked since no specific missing edge has been identified yet — this is an open question, not a confirmed gap).
- **engine.build edge — deliberately not filed.** Every sibling buildable-archetype module has a registered `engine.build` edge; destination does not. I considered filing this as a BUG-058 finding but held back (see Out of scope) because no §48 sentence names the construction-queue mechanic as explicitly as the traffic/shopping/logistics cases — flagging for Bill's judgement on whether the sibling-pattern argument alone (used successfully for finding #9's PoliciesAPI case) is sufficient here too.
- **ASM-326 (logged).** The regional-draw split/shared-machinery boundary between `engine.destination` and `engine.tourism` is this file's own architectural drawing, not spec-specified — see the assumption record.
