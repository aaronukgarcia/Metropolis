## This file's role (and the relationship to FEAT-055)

FEAT-055 (`feat.megafacilities`) is the §8 umbrella: its AC-4/AC-5 already own the **shared expert-workforce gate** (a numeric, data-sourced threshold on `engine.education`'s research-points output — ASM-512: "reads engine.education research-points, not raw skilled-citizen count"), and its AC-1 names "SpaceX/CERN/Aldermaston-class science/defence mega-facilities" as one of the three catalogue anchors. **This file does not re-specify that gate** (GR#3). It owns the spaceport's *facility-specific* behaviour — launch operations, the launch-exclusion blight, the FDI/tourism draw, prestige — and asserts that the shared gate is load-bearing *for this facility* (AC-3). The two files' boundary is the same "owns the gate shape vs owns the facility mechanics" split `feat.megafacilities.md` draws against `engine.education.md`. Whether FEAT-097 inherits the gate from FEAT-055 (its BOW deps do **not** list FEAT-055) or consumes `engine.education` directly through its own edge is an open registration decision — see ES-1/ES-2 and ASM-708.

## North-star vetting (dev-team-process.md v1.12 BA mandate)

- **Q4 (snowball) / Q5 (long bet).** The spaceport is the aerospace flavour of the education snowball's summit (FEAT-055's Q4/Q5, §10's own words). The gate is the payoff of decades of education funding; the build is a bet the player has been funding since the first university.
- **Q1/Q2 (conflicting demand / felt no).** Enormous capex + land + a permit + day-one decommission liability, and the *gate* means the money is only spendable at all if education was funded instead of everything else. Choosing a coastal site pays a blight price (Q2's felt no, in land-value terms).
- **Q3 (consequence sticks).** The launch-exclusion contour and the decommission liability are both persistent — the coastal site the player blights stays blighted, and the bill never goes away until restoration.

## Scope

The spaceport's facility-specific mechanics: launch scheduling as multi-year-build-then-operate events producing queryable export/prestige outputs; a launch-exclusion/noise contour (data-sourced radius + `blightClass`) that degrades nearby land value/amenity; FDI-prospect and tourism demand injection into `engine.fdi`/`engine.tourism`; a queryable prestige output; and the **inherited** gates — the shared expert-workforce gate (via FEAT-055/`engine.education`), the permit (FEAT-053), and day-one decommission liability (FEAT-054). ### Functional — the expert-workforce gate (the "money alone cannot buy it" mechanic)

- **AC-2 (GR#20; the gate verdict is consumed, not reimplemented — load-bearing for this facility).** The spaceport's build/operate path consumes the **shared** §8 expert-workforce gate — the numeric threshold on `engine.education`'s research-points output that FEAT-055 AC-4/AC-5 own — and there is no spaceport-local reimplementation of education accounting (no graduate count, no research-points computation, no "aerospace workforce" counter maintained inside the spaceport package). Check: `grep -rn "graduat\|researchPoint\|attainment\|workforce" internal/engine/spaceport/*.go` (excluding `_test.go`) finds no local education-output model; the gate verdict enters via either the FEAT-055 gate surface or a registered `feat.spaceport → engine.education` edge (see ES-1/ES-2 — the shape is a Bill decision, ASM-708). **Dependency note:** `engine.education` and FEAT-055's gate both have no code on disk; the precise gate-consumption method name is owned by FEAT-055/`engine.education.md`, not pinned here.
- **AC-3 (§8 "you cannot buy this with money alone" — money/DP/milestone are not sufficient, and the threshold flips the verdict).** A spaceport build attempt with ample treasury + development points + a qualifying milestone but an education output (research points) **below** the data-file threshold is **rejected**; raising **only** the education output above the threshold (all other inputs unchanged) flips the verdict to accepted. This is the load-bearing check that keeps the gate mechanical rather than a funds gate wearing an expert label. Check: a passing test seeds a rich-but-under-educated city state and asserts rejection, then raises only the education output above the `data/spaceport.json` threshold and asserts acceptance (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Mm]oney.*[Cc]antBuy\|func Test.*[Ee]xpert.*[Gg]ate\|func Test.*[Tt]hreshold.*[Ff]lip" internal/engine/spaceport/*_test.go`). **False-pass risk:** carrying a boolean `"research": true` unlock (the existing `space_launch_complex` shape) would satisfy a shallow "it is research-gated" read while money-alone-plus-one-unlock still buys it — the numeric-threshold flip test is what rejects the boolean shortcut (the exact failure mode FEAT-055 AC-4 names).

### Functional — launch operations (the facility's own mechanic)

- **AC-4 (§MP "launches (events, exports)"; deterministic multi-year-then-operate).** The spaceport, once built, produces a **schedule** of launches — a deterministic sequence of launch events (data-sourced cadence, GR#15) each of which, when it fires, produces a queryable export event and a queryable prestige increment. Launches do not fire before the build completes, and the build itself is a multi-year progression (the §MP "multi-year builds" characteristic) that cannot be completed by a single command. Check: a passing test builds the spaceport, advances the documented build duration, and asserts (a) zero launch events before completion, (b) a deterministic launch schedule matching the data-file cadence over N months, and (c) each fired launch increments a queryable export/prestige figure by the documented per-launch amount (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Ll]aunch.*[Ss]chedule\|func Test.*[Ee]xport.*[Ee]vent\|func Test.*[Mm]ultiYear.*[Bb]uild" internal/engine/spaceport/*_test.go`). **False-pass risk:** a "prestige" that is credited once at build-completion (a lump sum) rather than per-launch would satisfy a "prestige is nonzero" read while §MP's "launches (events, exports)" is an ongoing flow, not a one-shot — the check requires per-launch increments.

### Functional — launch-exclusion blight

- **AC-5 (§MP "noise blight (coastal siting realistic)"; the BOW's "launch-exclusion blight").** The spaceport carries a launch-exclusion/noise contour: a data-sourced exclusion radius (GR#15) within which nearby cells' land value/amenity is **degraded** — via `engine.world`'s decay overlay and/or `engine.finance`'s land-value surface — and the facility's `blightClass` reflects it. The contour is a real, queryable blight, not a cosmetic label on the catalogue entry. Check: a passing test places the spaceport and asserts a cell inside the exclusion radius has a worse land-value/amenity figure than the same cell with the spaceport absent (or a cell just outside the radius), with the radius read from `data/spaceport.json`, not hardcoded (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Ee]xclusion\|func Test.*[Bb]light.*[Rr]adius\|func Test.*[Nn]oiseContour" internal/engine/spaceport/*_test.go`). **False-pass risk:** setting `blightClass: "medium"` in the catalogue without any world/land-value effect would pass a "the entry says blight" grep while the coastal trade-off is purely cosmetic — the check requires the degraded-land-value assertion, mirroring the "real blight, not cosmetic" discipline of `engine.world`'s decay overlay.
