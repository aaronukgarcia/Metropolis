## The twenty-year fuse: how this file makes it checkable before twenty years pass

§27's own text ("quality shortfalls surface 10–20 game-years later... the longest fuse in the game") is exactly the shape of claim this project's testability standard treats as a warning, not a feature to admire. If the *only* thing an AC can check is "did the eventual skill-gap number come out right after a 20-year run", that AC will never actually run in CI, in a Tester pass, or in a junior's own dev loop — and a wrong implementation of the pipeline's early stages is indistinguishable from a correct one until the run nobody does finishes. So this file's design principle, applied throughout the AC list below rather than stated once and forgotten:

**Every mechanism whose *effect* is decades away has *intermediate state* that is not decades away, and that intermediate state is what gets checked.** Four such checkpoints, each independently verifiable within a handful of simulated months, not years:
1. **The pipeline's cohort-accounting identity (AC-11)** — every pupil is accounted for every month, checkable on month 2 of a synthetic run, regardless of what quality level was funded.
2. **Attainment is written at the moment of each stage transition, not deferred to a terminal event (AC-6)** — a citizen's attainment score after primary is a real, inspectable number the month primary ends, not a placeholder waiting for a 20-year payoff to compute retroactively.
3. **Personality drift is applied incrementally, per stage, in the documented direction, the month a stage's funding-quality is known (AC-7)** — the *sign and shape* of the twenty-year effect is verifiable from one stage's drift alone; only the *magnitude after many compounding stages* is what actually takes decades, and this module does not need to prove that magnitude to prove the mechanism is wired correctly.
4. **The Slow-Fuse projection obligation is discharged at the moment of the funding decision (AC-9), not at the moment the consequence lands** — this is A5's whole point, and it means the "is this checkable" question about a funding cut's *eventual* effect is answered by a projection existing *now*, not by simulating twenty years to see if the number matches.

None of the ACs below require a multi-decade headless run to pass. Where the spec's own claim genuinely is about a 10-20 year horizon (the felt magnitude of a skill-gap), that magnitude is explicitly out of scope for this item (see Out of scope) and is `engine.projections`' and later consumption/leisure modules' job to eventually demonstrate over real gameplay — this module's job, and the thing every AC below actually checks, is that the pipeline *producing* that eventual effect is correct at every step along the way.

## User stories

- **US-1.** As a month-aged citizen (via `engine.citizens`, a registered outbound dependency), I need to flow through the documented stage pipeline (nursery→primary→secondary→fork→university→adult education→U3A) gated by real capacity, funding-quality, and the September intake, so my educational trajectory is a real simulated pipeline, not a label.
- **US-2.** As `engine.season` (a registered outbound dependency, MOD-027), I need stage transitions gated on the real `IsSchoolIntakeMonth` September gate (per `engine.season.md` AC-7), so "the twenty-year education fuse advances on a realistic calendar, not continuously" (`engine.season.md`'s own US-4, naming this module).
- **US-3.** As `engine.traffic` (a registered outbound dependency), I need school-run trip generation (children walking/bussing to nursery/primary/secondary) to be real traffic demand, so "school-run traffic is real and awful" is mechanically true, not narrative.
- **US-4.** As `engine.services` (a registered outbound dependency, MOD-033), I need each stage's capacity and funding→quality to be this module registering against the shared service framework (per `engine.services.md`'s US-1 extensibility contract), not a duplicated capacity/coverage/quality model.
- **US-5.** As `engine.projections` (MOD-031, a sibling S9-adjacent module, not owned by this file), I need any education-funding decision whose principal effect lands more than 5 game-years out to be **submitted through the Slow-Fuse gate** `engine.projections` already builds (per `engine.projections.md` AC-5's generic, decision-type-agnostic mechanism) with a real projected-consequence payload attached — this module's obligation is to submit correctly-tagged decisions and a real projection payload; the gate's enforcement mechanics belong entirely to `engine.projections` and are not restated here.
- **US-6.** As the citizen record (§5.1, via `engine.citizens`), I need my attainment score and personality vector updated at each stage transition I pass through, in the documented direction (good schooling widens ambition/novelty-seeking/taste range, poor schooling narrows them), so the eventual workforce-skill and leisure-demand effects are literally traceable to my own education history, not a city-wide fudge factor.
- **US-7.** As `engine.invariant`, I need every pupil accounted for as a conserved stock the moment this module exists, so a pupil cannot silently vanish between stages or be double-counted across two stages at once.
- **US-8.** As university (a stage within this module), I need halls capacity to gate enrolment and research points to be a real, queryable output of graduating cohorts, so the eventual mega-project gate (§CS, out of scope here) has a real number to consume once its own module exists.

## Scope

The full stage pipeline (nursery, primary, secondary, sixth form / technical college / leave-at-16 fork, university, adult education, U3A/library) with per-stage capacity, funding→quality (via `engine.services`), September-gated transitions (via `engine.season`), school-run trip generation (via `engine.traffic`), attainment recording and personality drift on the citizen record (via `engine.citizens`) at each transition, university research-point production, and the cohort-conservation accounting for pupils as a new invariant-checker stock. This item submits education-funding decisions to `engine.projections`' Slow-Fuse gate; it does not build that gate.

### Functional — attainment and personality drift (the fuse's early-checkable mechanism)

- **AC-6 (§5.1/§27; the attainment-is-written-now check, not the eventual-effect check).** At the moment a citizen exits a stage (promotion, fork choice, or graduation), a quality-weighted attainment score for that stage is computed from the stage's actual realised funding-quality (via `engine.services`, not a flat per-stage constant) and written to the citizen's education record through `CitizensAPI`'s command-based mutation path (per `engine.citizens.md` AC-1b, never a direct field write) **immediately at the transition**, not deferred, batched, or computed retroactively when some later system asks for it. Check: `go doc ./internal/engine/education` shows an attainment-write call at the stage-transition boundary; a passing test transitions a citizen out of primary under two different funded-quality levels and asserts the written attainment score differs, measured *immediately after the transition tick*, with no further simulated time elapsed (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Aa]ttainment.*[Ww]rite\|func Test.*[Ii]mmediateAttainment" internal/engine/education/*_test.go`). **Lazy implementation this rejects — the exact "looks correct for twenty years" failure mode the brief names:** an implementation that stores only a stage-completion flag and defers attainment computation to whatever later module eventually reads it (computed lazily, on first read, using whatever funding-quality value happens to be current *then* rather than what was actually funded *at the time*) would satisfy "attainment exists on the record" on any inspection that happens to occur after the fact, while silently substituting present-day funding for historical funding — a bug invisible until a very specific, late read exposes it. Testing "attainment is written now, from now's quality" catches this at month 2, not year 20.
- **AC-7 (§5.1 personality drift; direction and incrementality, not magnitude).** Each stage transition applies a personality-drift delta to the citizen's 8-axis P vector (via `CitizensAPI`'s documented `ApplyEducationEffect`-or-equivalent, per `engine.citizens.md` AC-3) in the spec-documented direction — good (above-baseline) schooling-quality widens ambition/novelty-seeking/taste-range axes, poor (below-baseline) schooling narrows them — applied **incrementally at each stage's own transition**, never accumulated silently and applied once at a terminal "education complete" event. Check: `go doc ./internal/engine/education` shows a per-stage drift call distinct from AC-6's attainment write; a passing test transitions the *same* citizen through two consecutive stages under differing quality levels and asserts the P-vector delta after stage 1 is already nonzero and in the documented direction *before* stage 2 has occurred (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Dd]rift.*[Ii]ncremental\|func Test.*[Pp]ersonalityDrift" internal/engine/education/*_test.go`). **This is the check that makes the twenty-year claim testable at all:** the eventual "twenty years later, narrower demand" effect is the *compounding* of many small, individually-checkable increments — if each increment's sign and shape are correct (this AC), the compounding is a `engine.citizens`-owned arithmetic fact, not a new twenty-year-scale mechanism this module would otherwise be unable to prove short of an actual twenty-year run. Magnitude tuning (how many points per quality-unit) is out of scope (see Escalations).
- **AC-8 (§27 university halls, graduates, research points).** University enrolment is capped by halls capacity (a distinct capacity input from teaching capacity, both consumed via `engine.services`), and a graduating cohort produces a queryable research-points output at a documented (data-sourced, GR#15) rate per graduate, exported on `EducationAPI` for a future consumer (§CS mega-projects, out of scope here — see Escalations on the empty inbound-consumer list). Check: `go doc ./internal/engine/education` shows a halls-capacity accessor distinct from teaching capacity and a research-points accessor; a passing test asserts enrolment above halls capacity is rejected/queued even when teaching capacity has headroom, and that a graduating cohort of size N produces research points proportional to N at the documented rate (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Hh]allsCapacity\|func Test.*[Rr]esearchPoints" internal/engine/education/*_test.go`).

### Functional — conservation identity (the pupil stock; also the early-checkability spine)

- **AC-10 (cohort accounting — the strongest available check, and per this file's opening section, checkable from month 2).** For every stage and every accounting month, the following identity holds exactly: `EnrolledThisMonth == EnrolledLastMonth + Intake(new entrants this September gate, or transfers from a feeder stage) − Promoted(to the next stage) − ForkedOut(secondary's three-way exit, counted once total, not per branch) − DroppedOut(documented attrition path, if any) − Deceased(via engine.citizens' mortality) − EmigratedOrRelocated(via engine.citizens)`. Every right-hand term is independently sourced from its own event stream (intake from the September-gate transition event, promoted/forked from this module's own transition log, deceased/emigrated from `engine.citizens`' life-event stream) — none is computed as the identity's remainder. Check: a passing test runs a synthetic cohort through several September gates with a mix of promotions, fork outcomes, a synthetic death, and a synthetic emigration, independently sums each right-hand term from its own source, and asserts the sum reconciles exactly against the independently-tracked `EnrolledThisMonth` figure at every stage, every month (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Cc]ohortAccount\|func Test.*[Pp]upilConservation" internal/engine/education/*_test.go`). **Lazy implementation this rejects:** computing any one term (most likely `DroppedOut`, since it has no other module tracking it independently) as a balancing remainder would make the identity tautologically true regardless of whether pupils are actually being tracked correctly through the fork — indistinguishable from correct until a much later system (workforce statistics, `engine.firms`' skill-pool sizing) reads a silently-wrong enrolment figure. Sourcing every term independently, and checking the sum only after, is what makes this identity able to fail — and unlike the eventual skill-gap effect, this identity is checkable on the very first September gate a synthetic test drives through, which is exactly the "intermediate state visible early" answer this file's opening section commits to.

Until the tripwire fires: once unblocked, `grep -n "invariant\.Register" internal/engine/education/*.go` shows registration at boot; until then, `doc.go` states the block, citing BUG-058.

## Out of scope

- **The eventual 10-20 year magnitude of a funding decision's skill-gap/leisure-demand effect.** This item proves the pipeline that *produces* that effect is correct at every step (AC-6/AC-7/AC-10); demonstrating the compounded magnitude over real gameplay is a later, separate concern for whichever consumption/leisure/firms modules eventually read attainment and P at scale, and is explicitly not testable within this item's own package tests without an actual multi-decade run — which this file deliberately does not require (see opening section).
- `engine.projections`' own Slow-Fuse gate mechanics, curve registration/composition, and confidence/provenance model — this item only submits to it (AC-9); `engine.projections.md` owns the gate itself.
- `engine.citizens`' own attainment/P-vector storage, mortality, and life-event stream mechanics — this item only writes through `CitizensAPI`'s documented commands (AC-6/AC-7); it does not own the citizen record.
- `engine.services`' own capacity/coverage/funding→quality function — this item registers stages against it (AC-2) but does not reimplement it.
- §CS mega-project gating's consumption of research points, and the sixth-form/technical-college/university curricular content itself — out of scope; this item exports research points (AC-8) for a future consumer.
- Specific per-stage capacity figures, funding-quality curve shapes, drift-magnitude-per-quality-unit, and research-points-per-graduate rate — balance data pending M2 tuning (see Escalations).
