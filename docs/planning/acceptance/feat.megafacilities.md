## North-star vetting (dev-team-process.md v1.12 BA mandate)

This is a player-facing build choice and answers **all five questions**, with Q4 and Q5 named verbatim by the spec's own north-star section:

- **Q1 (conflicting demand, sharpened directly).** The THORP/CANDU-class plant is the ultimate capex claim (§10 "capex-hungry extraction vs everything else the pie funds"). But the sharper conflict is the gate itself: the expert-workforce threshold means the money spent on this plant is only spendable AT ALL if the player already diverted years of funding into education rather than everything else. The mega-facility is a bill you can only earn the right to pay.
- **Q2 (felt no, directly).** Choosing to build gives up enormous capital + land + a permit + a clean balance sheet (the day-one decommission liability, §7/§8). Choosing not to fund the education pipeline gives up the entire end-game tier — a felt no on the *twenty-year* scale.
- **Q3 (consequence sticks, directly).** The decommission liability accrues from build/permit time and never goes away; the education fuse means the gate cannot be rushed or bought.
- **Q4 (snowball, directly — this IS the summit).** §10's own words: "the expert workforce for end-game plants is the education snowball's summit." This item is that summit made buildable — it is the payoff that the education snowball (§4 Q4 of the brief) was climbing toward.
- **Q5 (long bet, directly).** §10's own words: "the university-to-reprocessing-plant pipeline" is the brief's worked example of a long bet. Building a mega-facility is stretching toward a goal the player has been funding since the first university opened.

## User stories

- **US-1.** As a player deep into the end-game whose education pipeline has been funded for decades, I need a THORP-reprocessing / CANDU-class nuclear plant as the ULTIMATE build tier, so the education long-bet pays off at civilisational scale (§8) rather than topping out at mid-game.
- **US-2.** As the player, I need the mega-facilities to be gated on a LARGE workforce of highly trained experts that I can only grow through the education system over time, so "you cannot buy this with money alone" is a real, mechanical constraint, not flavour text — no amount of cash, milestones, or development points substitutes for the educated workforce.
- **US-3.** As the player, I need SpaceX / CERN / Aldermaston-class science/defence mega-facilities to share that exact gate shape (expert-workforce-gated, permit-gated, enormous), so the end-game has distinct science and defence ambitions beyond the nuclear plant rather than three reskins of one building.
- **US-4.** As the operator of a working port, I need a Felixstowe-scale-or-larger container-port tier that genuinely upgrades my freight capacity, so "port exists" graduates to "deep-sea container terminal" (§8) as a real capacity step, not a renamed `container_terminal`.
- **US-5.** As the balance designer (Aaron, GR#15), I need every gate threshold, cost, build duration, and capacity figure in a data file I can edit without a rebuild, so end-game balance is tunable without touching gate or catalogue code.
- **US-6.** As `data.catalogue`/`buildings.json` (FEAT-010), I need the mega-facilities to EXTEND my existing mega-projects supplement rather than fork or replace it, so the catalogue schema stays single-source (§8 "extend ... rather than replacing it").

## Scope

The three end-game mega-facility classes as new catalogue entries in `buildings.json`'s existing `mega-projects` supplement (extend, don't fork); the **expert-workforce gate** — a numeric, data-sourced (GR#15) threshold on an `engine.education` output, checked via the already-registered `feat.megafacilities`→`engine.education` edge, which money/milestones/DP cannot satisfy (the "money alone can't buy it" mechanic); the **Felixstowe-class container-port tier** expressed through the already-registered `feat.megafacilities`→`engine.freight` edge (extending, not reimplementing, that module's port-capacity model); the permit and decommission-liability obligations inherited from §7 via `feat.facilitypermits`/`feat.decommission` (cross-referenced, not reimplemented). Does **not** include: the education lifecycle, attainment model, or research-points *production* itself (`engine.education.md` — this item only consumes its output); the freight port-capacity/customs/tonnage accounting itself (`engine.freight.md` — this item only adds a tier that reads through it); the milestone/DP/achievement/funds gate machinery (`engine.unlocks.md` — its scope explicitly excludes research points); the permit system's own routing and the decommission accrual/discharge mechanics (`feat.facilitypermits.md`/`feat.decommission.md`); the extraction ladder, deposit model, or refinement chains (sibling features in the same package).
