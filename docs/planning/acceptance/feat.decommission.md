## North-star vetting (dev-team-process.md v1.12 BA mandate)

This item is finance-side accounting infrastructure, not a player-facing screen, but it is the mechanism that makes one specific north-star consequence real, so the case is made rather than skipped:

- **Q3 (consequence sticks) — primary, and the reason the item exists.** The liability is accrued on day one and never leaves the books until restoration actually happens; the strip-miner who never restores carries the landscape bill for the entire run. A depleted mine, a demolished factory, an abandoned chemical works — none of them shed the cost by being ignored. This is the "full lifecycle priced in" property §7 states, and it is the exact Q3 consequence (a bill that always comes due) the BOW description itself names ("North-star: consequences stick").
- **Q1/Q2 (conflicting demand / felt no) — sharpened downstream, not by this surface alone.** The felt "no" of Q1/Q2 lands when the permit gate (FEAT-053) and the F2 balance-sheet screen (ui.screen.finance) are wired: choosing to permit/build a facility now visibly reduces balance-sheet headroom and worsens credit rating. This item only creates the liability that makes that no *felt*; it does not render it. Like `feat.resourcedeposits`, it is the honest infrastructure the entertainment is built on.
- **Q5 (long bet) — indirectly.** Building a facility whose restoration bill is priced in from day one is a bet against your own future ability to pay it — the liability is the "deferred payoff in reverse" that makes the permit/build decision a stretch rather than a free play.

## User stories

- **US-1.** As `engine.finance`'s balance sheet, I need every permitted facility's "put back to nature" cost recorded as a real liability at build/permit time — not first computed at closure — so the player sees the full lifecycle price of a facility from the day it is permitted, and closure is never a surprise bill.
- **US-2.** As the credit-rating machinery (`engine.finance.md` AC-5), I need the outstanding decommission liability to count as debt-like exposure in the debt/revenue denominator, so a liability-heavy estate is real risk (worse rating, harder credit) rather than a cosmetic number on a screen.
- **US-3.** As the restoration mechanic (`engine.mining.md` AC-9's `Reclaim`, when built), I need a discharge surface that settles the liability when restoration completes, so the bill is paid, not forgotten — and the estate's books actually clear.
- **US-4.** As an operator growing a facility, I need the liability to grow with it, so a facility expanded under the permit gate re-accrues a larger restoration bill rather than keeping the smaller one it started with.
- **US-5.** As `ui.screen.finance` (the F2 balance sheet), I need the decommission liability figure to drill through to its ledger lines via the same traceability `engine.finance.md` AC-11 already specifies, so the number is auditable, not an opaque blob.

## Scope

The decommissioning-liability accounting surface on the shared `internal/engine/finance` package: the data-sourced (GR#15) "put back to nature" cost computation keyed by facility type + size/growth stage; the double-entry accrual that books the liability to the ledger at build/permit time and keeps it on the books until discharge; growth-indexed re-accrual (the delta, not a re-book); the discharge surface the restoration mechanic invokes; credit-rating exposure integration via `engine.finance.md` AC-5; and drill-through via AC-11. Does **not** include: the permit gate itself (FEAT-053/`feat.facilitypermits` — the unbuilt caller that invokes the accrual); the facility-construction/build hook (`engine.build`/MOD-026); the restoration/reclamation mechanics (`engine.mining.md` AC-9 `Reclaim` — the unbuilt caller that invokes the discharge); the land-value/decay recovery that restoration produces (`engine.mining`'s blight-removal effect feeding `engine.finance.md` AC-4 `LandPrice` and `engine.world`'s decay overlay — a consequence of the restoration mechanic, not of the liability accounting); the facility-type taxonomy itself (`data/buildings.json`/FEAT-010); or any exact £ cost magnitude (balance-number regime).

## Acceptance criteria

### Functional — the liability surface & data-sourced cost

- **AC-1 (GR#20; shared-package surface, unpopulated inbound contract — mirrors `feat.resourcedeposits.md` AC-1's pattern).** A decommission-liability surface exists on the shared `internal/engine/finance` package, distinct from `engine.finance.md` AC-1's money-flow ledger/loan/insolvency surface, exposing at minimum: compute liability, accrue (book) liability, query outstanding liability, adjust (re-accrue) liability on growth, and discharge liability. code.json's `feat.decommission` inbound contract is unpopulated (`consumers: []` — nothing has registered to call INTO this feature), so, like `feat.resourcedeposits.md` AC-1, the feature surfaces through the shared package rather than a separate inbound contract. **The facility reference the surface takes is a data-file-defined facility-type key plus a size/growth stage — NOT a cross-package import of any facility module's concrete struct.** `feat.decommission`'s only registered outbound edge is `engine.finance`; it has no edge to `engine.mining` or `engine.build`, so under GR#20 its surface cannot depend on those modules' types (a mining-specific `Mine` or build-specific `Facility` struct would be an illegal unregistered import). Check: `go doc ./internal/engine/finance` shows a decommission-liability function/type set distinct from `FinanceAPI`'s `Ledger`/`CreditRating`/`IsInsolvent` accessors; `internal/engine/finance/doc.go` notes the shared-package, no-separate-inbound-contract arrangement explicitly (`grep -n "feat.decommission" internal/engine/finance/doc.go`).
- **AC-2 (GR#15 — the "put back to nature" cost is data-sourced, never a code constant).** `ComputeDecommissionLiability(facilityTypeKey, sizeStage) (Money, error)` (or equivalent) computes the restoration cost from a data file (e.g. `data/decommission.json`, following the `data/*.json` convention already used by `errors.json`/`market.json`/`buildings.json` and the `data/deposits.json`/`data/extraction.json` proposals in the sibling files), keyed by facility type + size/growth stage, and every numeric cost parameter traces to a load from that file — none hardcoded as a Go literal in the implementation. Check: `grep -rn "0\.[0-9]\+\|[0-9]\{2,\}" internal/engine/finance/decommission*.go` (excluding `_test.go`, array indices, and enum ordinals) shows no bare balance-shaped numeric literals tied to a restoration cost; `go doc ./internal/engine/finance` or `internal/engine/finance/doc.go` names the data file path explicitly; a passing test edits a cost parameter in the data file between two test runs with the SAME `(facilityTypeKey, sizeStage)` input and asserts the computed liability changes, proving the parameter is actually read rather than merely present in a file nobody loads (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Dd]ataDriven.*[Dd]ecomm\|func Test.*[Cc]ostProvenance" internal/engine/finance/*_test.go`).
- **AC-3 (the open BA question from the BOW description, answered affirmatively — liability indexes with growth).** The computed liability is strictly positive for every facility type at every growth stage, and monotonically non-decreasing as the size/growth stage increases — a bigger facility carries a bigger restoration bill, never a smaller one, and never zero or negative. This answers the BOW description's own question ("does the liability index with facility growth — it should, expansion under the permit gate is the natural accrual point") in the affirmative, mechanically rather than in prose. Check: a passing test loads the full data-file taxonomy and asserts, for every facility type, liability > 0 at every stage AND non-decreasing across the ordered stage sequence (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Gg]rowthIndex\|func Test.*[Ll]iabilityMonotonic\|func Test.*[Nn]onZeroLiability" internal/engine/finance/*_test.go`). **False-pass risk:** a data file that lists plausible-looking numbers but is never checked for the ordering property could accidentally (or via a careless balance edit) produce a "bigger facility, smaller bill" row — the monotonic check is what catches that at data-load time rather than in a much later balance review.

### Functional — the accrual ("accrued at build/permit time, not a surprise at closure")

- **AC-4 (the headline property — the liability is on the books from accrual, never computed only at closure).** `AccrueDecommissionLiability(facilityTypeKey, sizeStage, facilityID) (LiabilityID, error)` posts a balanced double-entry transaction to `engine.finance.md` AC-1's ledger, crediting a distinct liability/provision account (distinct from the income/expense flow accounts and from the debt/loan accounts) in the computed amount (AC-2), and the outstanding liability is queryable from that moment onward — there is no code path that first materialises the cost at closure time. The feature exposes this accrual surface but does **not** own the trigger: the caller (the permit grant, FEAT-053; or the build hook, MOD-026) is unbuilt and unregistered, per the open-dependency note in Escalations. Check: a passing test accrues a facility and asserts (a) the posted transaction has balanced debit+credit legs summing to zero (reusing `engine.finance.md` AC-1's own double-entry balance check), (b) `OutstandingDecommissionLiability(facilityID)` returns exactly the computed amount immediately after accrual and before any restoration, and (c) the liability is booked to a distinct liability/provision account category, not a flow account (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Aa]ccru.*[Ll]iability\|func Test.*[Dd]oubleEntry.*[Dd]ecomm" internal/engine/finance/*_test.go`).
- **AC-5 (growth re-accrual — the delta is posted, not the full amount re-booked).** `AdjustDecommissionLiability(facilityTypeKey, newSizeStage, facilityID)` (or equivalent) updates the booked liability when a facility grows, posting the **delta** between the new computed liability (AC-2/AC-3) and the currently-booked liability to the ledger — a facility grown from stage N to stage N+1 has its outstanding liability increase by exactly `compute(N+1) − compute(N)`, never re-booked from zero and never doubled. Check: a passing test accrues at stage N, re-accrues at stage N+1, and asserts the outstanding liability equals `compute(N+1)` and that the newly posted ledger entry's amount equals the delta, not the full recomputed value (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Rr]eaccru\|func Test.*[Aa]djust.*[Ll]iability" internal/engine/finance/*_test.go`).

### Functional — queryability & the discharge

- **AC-6 (drill-through — reuses `engine.finance.md` AC-11, not a new traceability mechanism).** The outstanding decommission-liability aggregate carries a line-retrieval method (reusing `engine.finance.md` AC-11's `Lines(aggregateID)` traceability surface, not a parallel decommission-owned ledger) that returns the underlying ledger entries composing it, summing to the reported aggregate. Check: a passing test accrues a liability, retrieves its lines via the same traceability surface AC-11 specifies, and asserts the retrieved lines sum to the reported outstanding liability (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Dd]rillThrough.*[Dd]ecomm\|func Test.*[Ll]iabilityLines" internal/engine/finance/*_test.go`).
- **AC-7 (discharge on restoration completion — cross-reference, not a new restoration mechanic).** `DischargeDecommissionLiability(facilityID) (Money, error)` settles the booked liability when restoration completes, posting a balanced discharge transaction (Dr liability, Cr cash/restoration-opex) that reduces the outstanding liability to zero and returns the settled amount. This file does **not** reimplement restoration: the restoration-completion event is `engine.mining.md` AC-9's `Reclaim` (unbuilt), and this feature only exposes the discharge surface that restoration will invoke — the "land value/decay on restoration" interaction the brief §9 names is the restoration mechanic's own downstream effect (blight removal feeding `engine.finance.md` AC-4 `LandPrice`), not this feature's job. Check: a passing test accrues then discharges and asserts (a) outstanding liability → 0, and (b) total money is conserved across the accrual+discharge pair (the settled amount moved from cash to the liability account and back, net zero) (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Dd]ischarge" internal/engine/finance/*_test.go`).

### Functional — the "real risk" integration (insolvency/credit-rating)

- **AC-8 (credit-rating exposure — "a liability-heavy estate is real risk", mirrors `engine.finance.md` FEAT-057 AC-25's pattern).** The outstanding decommission liability counts as debt-like exposure in `engine.finance.md` AC-5's `CreditRating` debt/revenue denominator — a city carrying a large accrued decommission liability has a same-or-worse credit rating than an otherwise-identical city with none, never better. It is **not** added to AC-7's monthly "obligations that must be met" insolvency set (a provision is a long-term liability, not a current-month payment): the insolvency risk is transmitted through the credit-rating channel, not by converting the liability into a monthly payment. Check: a passing test computes `CreditRating` for two synthetic city states identical except that one carries an accrued decommission liability, and asserts the liability-bearing city's rating is same-or-worse, never better (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Cc]reditRating.*[Dd]ecomm" internal/engine/finance/*_test.go`). **False-pass risk (the AC an attacker should try hardest to break):** an implementation that books the liability to a cosmetic off-balance-sheet field never fed into `CreditRating` would satisfy a shallow "a liability number exists somewhere" read while defeating the "real risk" property — the check must prove the liability reaches the credit-rating denominator, not merely that it is queryable (exactly the FEAT-057 AC-25 off-balance-sheet-PFI lesson, applied here).

### Error handling

- **AC-9 (GR#7 — malformed data and invalid operations).** Loading the decommission data file when it is missing, malformed (invalid JSON), or invalid (a negative cost, a zero cost, a monotonic-violating stage sequence per AC-3, an unknown facility-type key), accruing a liability for a facility-type key absent from the data file, or discharging a `facilityID` with no outstanding liability, each produce a registry-sourced error (new `MET-E`-range code) rather than a silent no-op, a zero-valued liability, or a partial ledger booking. The AC-3 monotonicity property is additionally enforced as a **load-time gate** (the loader rejects a violating file), not merely as a test assertion. Check: `grep -n "MET-" internal/engine/finance/decommission*.go` finds registry code references on the data-loading, accrual, and discharge paths; passing tests cover the named cases and, per the BUG-100 convention, assert the returned error's registry code matches AND that no ledger entry was posted or mutated as a side effect, not merely that a matching-named test function exists (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Dd]ecomm.*[Ii]nvalid\|func Test.*[Dd]oubleDischarge\|func Test.*[Mm]alformedDecomm" internal/engine/finance/*_test.go`).

### Determinism, safety & scale

- **AC-10 (GR#21 — pure function, no wall clock, no RNG).** `grep -rn "time.Now\|time.Since" internal/engine/finance/decommission*.go` (excluding `_test.go`) returns no matches, and `grep -n "rand.New\|math/rand\"" internal/engine/finance/decommission*.go` (excluding `_test.go`) finds no RNG source — the liability is a pure function of `(facilityTypeKey, sizeStage, data file)`, deterministic by construction, never wall clock and never a stochastic draw.
- **AC-11 (int64 micro-pounds — reuses `engine.finance.md` AC-2).** All decommission-liability monetary values use `engine.finance.md` AC-2's `int64` micro-pounds `Money` type (1 GBP = 1,000,000 micro-pounds or the documented equivalent fixed-point scale) — no `float32`/`float64` monetary field. Check: `grep -n "float32\|float64" internal/engine/finance/decommission*.go` (excluding `_test.go` and comments) finds no matches on monetary fields.
- **AC-12 (race safety — scoped subset).** `go test ./internal/engine/finance/... -race -count=1 -run Decommission` passes with no data race (scoped to the decommission subset via `-run`, since the shared package also carries `engine.finance.md`'s own AC-16 race suite).
