# Acceptance criteria — feat.pharmacampus (FEAT-101)

**BOW code:** FEAT-101 (P2, open) — "Pharma/R&D campus (Pfizer-class)."
**Spec refs:** §46 Multinational Attraction & Anchor Employers (`docs/METROPOLIS-MASTER-v2.1.md` lines 627-639: the M7+ prospect/competitive-bid/anchor mechanic; the archetype table row — "Pharma R&D campus ('Pfizer' — *Sandwich, Kent is the real precedent*) | university, graduates, clean utilities | labs: high-wage white collar, low freight"; the win condition "thousands of direct jobs, supply-chain firms spawning around it (§45 gets a demand injection), university links, exports, reputation"; the risk list "dependency (an anchor closing is a §32-coal-mine-scale shock — one-employer towns are fragile by design)"); §45 (§45 demand injection — the supply-chain-firm spawning path); §27 the educational lifecycle (university → graduates + research points — the education long bet, "the longest fuse in the game"); §32 (line 460 — the coal-mine-scale closure shock and the blight model); §54/line 680 (FDI as one of the ways "money enters the city economy"); `docs/planning/resources-design-brief.md` §6 (the "Groton-Pfizer-class pharma/R&D campus — Pfizer's real UK campus was Sandwich, Kent — in-map; a lovely FDI-anchor tie-in" archetype), §7 (permit + decommission liability for every large facility); code.json (unregistered — see Escalations); `data/buildings.json` (the existing thin `pharma_r_d_campus` row: `supplementCategory: "fdi-anchors"`, `section: "SUP3"`, `blightClass: "none"`, `unlock.milestone: "M7"`); `docs/planning/acceptance/feat.commoditymarket.md` (FEAT-052, whose AC-5 owns the pharma campus **manufacture-side** ChemAPI chain stage — ASM-488/ASM-696); `docs/planning/acceptance/engine.fdi.md` (MOD-059, whose prospect/bid/anchor-registration/closure machinery this feature consumes); `docs/planning/acceptance/engine.education.md` (MOD-041, whose graduates/research-points output this feature consumes and feeds).
**Design (Aaron, 2026-08-15, BOW FEAT-101):** a Groton-Pfizer-class pharma/R&D campus as a **distinct modelled facility**, not a generic anchor row — thousands of skilled high-wage white-collar jobs, low freight, clean-utility needs — whose long bet is **education + FDI compounding**: a city that funds education produces graduates/research that make the pharma prospect winnable and the won campus more valuable, and the won campus in turn feeds graduate/research demand back into education. The loop, not the single transaction, is the point.
**This file's role:** this is the **facility-catalogue + wiring** feature of the FDI-anchor cluster (sibling of FEAT-103 mine types / FEAT-104 farm types / FEAT-105 factory types). It turns the existing thin `pharma_r_d_campus` catalogue row into a typed, data-sourced facility and wires the education↔FDI compounding loop, supply-chain spawning, exports, and the permit/decommission obligations. It does **not** re-specify MOD-059's prospect/bid/incentive/anchor-registration mechanics (this feature supplies the education-output *term* into that machinery), FEAT-052's manufacture-side chain stage (ASM-488/ASM-696), or MOD-041's education pipeline (this feature only consumes graduates/research and emits graduate/research demand).
**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Status:** draft-ahead — the feature has **no code.json registration** and **no master-plan entry** (see Escalations), and all four of its real dependencies (FEAT-053, MOD-041, MOD-059, plus the FEAT-054 decommission inheritance implied by §7) are open with no code. Directional numbers only, per the balance-number regime below.
**Package under test:** `internal/engine/fdi/` (assumed home shared with `engine.fdi`/MOD-059, pending Bill's module-vs-feature ruling — ASM-695).
**Balance-number regime (standing rule, same convention as `engine.fdi.md` ASM-310 / `feat.megafacilities.md` AC-3):** every capex/opex/throughput/jobs/utility-draw/blight/export figure for the pharma campus is a **placeholder** in a data file (`data/pharmacampus.json`, ASM-700), each carrying a disclosure comment naming it pending Aaron's balance pass. No AC may be satisfied by a junior inventing a final jobs or export figure; every AC touching a figure requires the value to live in the data file and requires tests to check **direction/structure** (higher education output → strictly better bid outcome; a won campus → nonzero graduate demand; jobs scale with facility size) rather than pinning a specific magnitude.

## North-star vetting (dev-team-process.md v1.12 BA mandate)

- **Q5 (long bet) — the headline, named by the BOW description itself.** "Education + FDI compounding into a high-value anchor employer" is the design's own words. The bet is two-directional and compounding: fund education now (a cost with a decades-late payoff, §27's "longest fuse") so that when the pharma prospect appears at M7+ the bid is winnable, and the won anchor then feeds education demand back. AC-3 (education → bid) and AC-4 (campus → education demand) are the two legs; each is directional, and the bet's losing side is real (an under-educated city loses the prospect — AC-3's lose branch — and a won anchor can close, AC-6's §32 shock).
- **Q1 (conflicting demand) — sharpened.** Every pound sunk into education *for the sake of* this anchor is a pound not spent on the pie's other slices; and the FDI incentive package itself (§46) costs real money before any benefit lands. This feature doesn't re-specify either spend — it makes their *payoff* (a winnable, valuable anchor) concrete and directional.
- **Q3 (consequence sticks) — the closure shock + decommission.** The won campus is a real firm whose closure is the §32 coal-mine-scale shock (one-employer town), and as a permitted large facility it carries the day-one decommission liability (AC-7). Both are inherited from MOD-059 / FEAT-054, not reimplemented.
- **Q4 (snowball) — the supply-chain + exports.** A won campus spawns supply-chain firms (§45) and exports; the anchor is a growth engine, not a static jobs number.

## User stories

- **US-1.** As a player who has funded education, I need the pharma-campus FDI prospect's bid outcome to be genuinely improved by my education output (graduates/research), so "education + FDI compounding" is a real loop I can invest in, not narration.
- **US-2.** As the won pharma campus, I need to be a distinct, data-sourced facility with its own footprint, output, jobs, and utility draw (labs: high-wage white collar, low freight, clean utilities — §46), not one shared "anchor" row wearing a name.
- **US-3.** As `engine.firms`/§45, I need supply-chain firms to spawn around the won campus through the real firm demand-injection path, so the anchor's footprint extends beyond its own direct jobs.
- **US-4.** As `engine.education`, I need the campus's presence to feed real graduate/research demand back into the education system, so the compounding loop has a second leg (education → FDI → education) rather than stopping at the win.
- **US-5.** As the balance-of-trade screen, I need the campus's exports to be a real, queryable flow, so "high-value anchor employer + exports" is measurable.
- **US-6.** As the player placing it, I need the campus permit-gated (FEAT-053) and decommission-liability-carrying (FEAT-054), so it is a real lifecycle commitment like every other large facility.

## Scope

The `pharma_r_d_campus` catalogue row enriched into a distinct, typed, data-sourced facility (footprint/output/jobs/utility-draw); the education↔FDI compounding wiring (education output into MOD-059's bid resolution; graduate/research demand out to MOD-041); supply-chain spawning via `engine.firms`; exports as a real trade flow; and the permit (FEAT-053) / decommission (FEAT-054) inheritance. Does **not** include: MOD-059's prospect generation, competitive-bid resolution, incentive-package composition, anchor-registration-as-firm, and closure-event mechanics (this feature supplies the education term and reads the outcome); FEAT-052's pharma **manufacture-side** ChemAPI chain stage (ASM-488/ASM-696); MOD-041's education pipeline, attainment, and research-point production (this feature only consumes and feeds it); or FEAT-053/FEAT-054's permit/decommission mechanics.

Its `blightClass`, `unlock`, and `section` remain single-sourced in `data/buildings.json` (GR#3: this feature does not re-declare them). Check: `grep -n "pharma_r_d_campus" data/buildings.json` still resolves the one existing row; `go doc ./internal/engine/fdi` (or the feature's package doc) shows the pharma campus as a distinct facility type with its own footprint/output/jobs/utility-draw fields; a passing test asserts the pharma campus and the `chemicals_complex` FDI-anchor resolve to **distinct** behavioural profiles (different jobs character and utility draw — labs vs heavy industry, per §46's "high-wage white collar, low freight" vs "blight class high") (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Pp]harma.*[Dd]istinct\|func Test.*[Aa]nchor.*[Pp]rofiles" internal/engine/fdi/*_test.go`). **False-pass risk:** a build that leaves the thin catalogue row alone and puts a "pharma" label on a generic anchor struct would pass a shallow "a pharma campus exists" read while providing none of the per-type modelling the feature's "distinct modelled facility" mandate (FEAT-103/104/105's shared phrasing) requires — the check requires the per-type fields to exist and differ from the other anchor's.
- **AC-2 (GR#15; balance-number placeholders — every figure in the data file, none in code).** The pharma campus's capex/opex/throughput/jobs/utility-draw/export figures are all sourced from a data file (`data/pharmacampus.json`, ASM-700), none hardcoded as Go numeric literals, and each numeric entry carries a non-empty placeholder/pending-tuning disclosure field per the balance-number regime (`data/market.json`'s "pending M2 Batch tuning" convention). Check: `grep -rn "0\.[0-9]\+\|[0-9]\{2,\}" internal/engine/fdi/pharma*.go` (excluding `_test.go` and array indices/enum ordinals) shows no bare balance-shaped numeric literals; the package doc names the data file path explicitly; a passing test edits a jobs figure in the data file between two runs with the same seed and asserts the facility's derived jobs figure changes (proving the parameter is actually read, not merely present in a file nobody loads) (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Pp]harma.*[Dd]ataDriven\|func Test.*[Dd]ataDriven.*[Pp]harma" internal/engine/fdi/*_test.go`).

### Functional — the long bet (education + FDI compounding)

- **AC-3 (the first leg — education output improves the pharma bid, directionally, not a pinned value).** The pharma prospect's bid resolution consumes an education-output term (graduates/research points, read via the registered `EducationAPI` edge — ASM-698 for which exact output) such that a city with **higher** education output wins the pharma prospect (or wins on strictly better terms) than an **otherwise-identical** city with lower output. The mechanism must admit both outcomes from real state — a sufficiently under-educated city loses the prospect to the off-map competing region — so the long bet has a losing side. This feature **supplies the education term into MOD-059's competitive-bid machinery through the registered edge**; it does not reimplement prospect generation or bid resolution. Check: a passing directional test holds every city-state input identical except the education output and asserts the higher-education city's pharma-bid outcome is strictly better, and a second assertion drives a deliberately-low education output and asserts the bid loses or degrades (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Ee]ducation.*[Bb]id\|func Test.*[Bb]id.*[Ee]ducation\|func Test.*[Cc]ompounding.*[Ff]irstLeg" internal/engine/fdi/*_test.go`); `grep -n "education\." internal/engine/fdi/pharma*.go` (excluding `_test.go`) shows the call into the registered education edge, and `grep -rn "prospect\|bid" internal/engine/fdi/pharma*.go` shows no local prospect/bid resolver reimplementation. **False-pass risk:** a build that hardcodes "pharma bid always wins once the player has a university" (a boolean, not a threshold/term) would pass a "education helps the bid" reading while being a one-way door — the check requires the **lose branch to be reachable** from low education output, the same defect-catcher `engine.fdi.md` AC-3 applies to its own competitive bid.
- **AC-4 (the second leg — the won campus feeds graduate/research demand, closing the loop).** Once the campus is a won anchor, it generates real, queryable graduate/research demand through the registered education edge (`EducationAPI`), scaling monotonically with the campus's own employment — so the compounding loop is **two-directional** (education → FDI → education), not one-way. Check: a passing directional test wins the campus and asserts the graduate/research demand registered to the education system is nonzero and non-decreasing in the campus's employment figure (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Cc]ampus.*[Dd]emand\|func Test.*[Ss]econdLeg\|func Test.*[Ll]oop.*[Cc]losed" internal/engine/fdi/*_test.go`); `grep -n "education\." internal/engine/fdi/pharma*.go` shows the demand-emitting call. **False-pass risk:** an anchor that records "university links" as a flavour string with no education-system demand would pass a shallow "the anchor has university links" read while the loop never closes — the check requires a real demand emission through the registered edge.
- **AC-5 (supply-chain spawning via `engine.firms` — §45 demand injection, GR#20).** On anchor win, supply-chain firms spawn around the campus through `engine.firms`' registered demand-injection path (§45's "supply-chain firms spawning around it"), not a pharma-local firm registry or a bespoke spawn list (ASM-699). Check: `grep -n "firms\." internal/engine/fdi/pharma*.go` (excluding `_test.go`) shows the call into the registered firms edge; `grep -rn "supplyChain\|spawn\|RegisterFirm" internal/engine/fdi/pharma*.go` shows no pharma-local firm-storage struct; a passing directional test asserts the number of spawned supply-chain firms is non-decreasing in the campus's employment (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Ss]upplyChain.*[Ss]pawn\|func Test.*[Dd]emandInjection" internal/engine/fdi/*_test.go`). **This is the same mechanism, not a scripted separate one** — mirroring `engine.fdi.md` AC-5/AC-6's precedent applied to supply-chain spawning rather than anchor registration.
- **AC-6 (jobs + exports real and queryable; closure reachable through the same firm path).** The campus's employment is a real `FirmsAPI` firm with skilled, high-wage white-collar jobs consistent with the §46 sheet ("thousands of direct jobs"; "labs: high-wage white collar"), its exports are a real queryable trade flow (through the registered trade/freight edge, not a pharma-local export counter), and its closure is reachable through `FirmsAPI`'s own closure path (the §32 one-employer-town shock) — cross-referenced to `engine.fdi.md` AC-5/AC-6, not reimplemented here. Check: a passing test asserts the won campus's firm is independently retrievable through `FirmsAPI` with nonzero employment, that its export figure is queryable through the registered trade surface, and that driving the firm to close via `FirmsAPI`'s own path is observable (the shock is reachable) (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Pp]harma.*[Ff]irm\|func Test.*[Aa]nchor.*[Cc]losure\|func Test.*[Ee]xport.*[Ff]low" internal/engine/fdi/*_test.go`).

### Error handling

- **AC-8 (GR#7).** Building or operating the pharma campus against an unknown facility key, a malformed data file (missing jobs figure, negative utility draw, an unrecognised archetype name), or a bid whose education term references an unregistered education output, produces a registry-sourced error (new `MET-E`-range code) and no partial state (no facility created, no firm registered, no demand emitted) — never a panic or a silently-created zero-value facility. Check: `grep -n "MET-" internal/engine/fdi/pharma*.go` finds registry code references; passing test coverage asserts the returned error's registry code matches AND that no facility/firm/demand state was mutated on failure, not merely that a matching-named test function exists (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Uu]nknownPharma\|func Test.*[Mm]alformedPharma\|func Test.*[Pp]harma.*[Rr]eject" internal/engine/fdi/*_test.go`).

### Determinism & safety

- **AC-9 (GR#21).** Facility-profile derivation, the education-term contribution to bid resolution, supply-chain spawning, and export accounting are deterministic functions of `(worldSeed, tick, prior state, loaded data, commands)` — never wall clock and never a shared/global RNG source. Check: `grep -rn "time.Now\|time.Since\|rand.New\|math/rand\"" internal/engine/fdi/pharma*.go` (excluding `_test.go`) returns no matches; a passing determinism test runs an identical win sequence twice and asserts byte-identical facility/firm/demand state, and asserts a different seed produces a measurably different outcome set (proving the seed is actually consumed) (`grep -rn "func Test.*[Dd]eterminis.*[Pp]harma\|func Test.*[Pp]harma.*[Dd]eterminis" internal/engine/fdi/*_test.go`).
- **AC-10 (GR#16; type-safe storage — int64 micro-pounds + saturating arithmetic).** All monetary and job-count arithmetic for the pharma campus uses `engine.finance`'s `int64` micro-pounds `Money` type and the project's saturating helpers (`foundation/num`) — no `float32`/`float64` monetary or job field, and no overflow-prone bare integer accumulation. Check: `grep -n "float32\|float64" internal/engine/fdi/pharma*.go` (excluding `_test.go` and comments) finds no monetary/job field; `grep -n "foundation/num\|satAdd\|sat\.Add" internal/engine/fdi/pharma*.go` shows the saturating-arithmetic route matching `engine.finance.md` AC-2 and `engine.maintenance.md` AC-6's standard.
