## 1. Context and goal

- **BOW:** MOD-066 (OPEN, P1, `engine.fiscal`, `internal/engine/fiscal/`, 10 files; deps MOD-022 finance, MOD-033 services, MOD-052 tax, all done). Note: the BOW item is still `open` although the package exists with tests: the lead should check whether a destructive verdict was ever recorded before treating it as built (`node claude-bow.js show MOD-066` shows none in its header). A new wiring item must be filed by the lead; this lane writes no BOW.
- **Northstar stage:** Baseline One to dogfood: the F2 master money view ("where the money goes") is the player's window onto the whole economy.
- **THE OVERLAP QUESTION (this pack's first duty): what does `engine.fiscal` add over the wired `engine.finance`?** Verified by reading both packages (2026-10-30):
  - **`engine.finance` (wired, MOD-022)** is the ledger: double-entry `Post`, accounts (treasury, households, firms, reserves, debt, external), `CollectTax`, `SettleOpex`, credit, insolvency, `MoneyStock`, `Lines`/`LinesByCategory`. In compose it feeds the `f2.finance` view whose only sub-view is the **balance sheet** (`finance_publish.go`: "balanceSheet-only", P&L/loans/creditRating as fields on it).
  - **`engine.fiscal` (unwired, MOD-066)** states in `doc.go` that it "owns NO independent money total": every figure is recomputed at query time from finance's ledger and `engine.tax`'s instruments. What it ADDS is exactly five things: (1) **Sankey topology** (`SankeyTopology`, `Node`, `MoneyIn`, `MoneyOut`, `BudgetBalance`, `DrillThrough`; money-in producers export/tourism/fdi/grants are **Provisional zero nodes**, never fabricated); (2) **tax breakdown by type** (`TaxBreakdown`, needs `engine.tax`); (3) **civil-service truth** (`SetCivilServiceWageBill`, gross vs net of income-tax clawback); (4) **municipality-as-department** (`SetPlanningFunding` -> `PermitSpeedMultiplier`, `BuildCostErrorRate`, `LayoutQualityBonus`, `CorruptionRisk`, curves in `data/fiscal.json`) and the childcare net line; (5) **benefit postings** (`PostUnemploymentSupport`, `PostHousingBenefit`, `PostSocialHousingBuild`): the ONLY fiscal functions that WRITE to the ledger, as treasury -> households transfers under categories `benefits.unemployment` and `benefits.housing`.
  - So the overlap verdict (AC-1) is: **it is a read-model over finance plus a small write-surface, not a competing ledger. Nothing in finance merges into fiscal and nothing in fiscal replaces finance.** What MERGES: the Sankey query is the second sub-view of the existing `f2.finance` view (no new money anywhere). What WIRES as a new module: the municipality curves (need a consumer), the benefit postings (need a driver), civil-service/childcare (need inputs). The developer must confirm or overturn each statement above in AC-1's artefact before building on it.
- **Further facts that shape scope (verified):**
  1. **`engine.tax` and `engine.projections` are unwired** (only unwired packages import them), and fiscal needs `SetTax`. Compose collects tax today via `finance.CollectTax(finance.TaxRates{IncomeRate: incomeNITaxRateBp}, ...)` with a constant rate. So `TaxBreakdown` over a `TaxAPI` that nobody posts through would show zeros. Decision needed (Q1): either tax revenue posting moves to `TaxAPI` (a larger change touching the monthly finance hook and the converge-fixture flows), or `TaxBreakdown` is derived from the ledger's own `tax.*` categories only. This pack assumes the second (smaller, honest) path and states it as a disclosure line; Aaron may overrule.
  2. **Municipality outputs have no Go consumer.** `engine/build` has no reference to permit speed, build-cost error, layout bonus or corruption (grep clean). Wiring `SetPlanningFunding` without a consumer is the built-but-not-wired defect. This pack therefore requires ONE consumer (AC-6) or an explicit deferral.
  3. **The TS twin exists.** `webconsole/src/sim/sankey.ts` builds a Sankey from `SimState.lastFlows`; `webconsole/src/sim/fiscal.ts` carries the TS fiscal constants. The browser's F2 view today is the TS path. Which side is the SSOT for the player-visible Sankey is Q2.
  4. **Benefits ownership split with social** (`wire-engine.social.md` AC-6): fiscal owns unemployment and housing benefit; social owns its caseload support category. No category may be posted twice.
- Prior rulings to look up: FEAT-231 "one money" (`onemoney_guard_test.go`), ASM on the engine.fiscal -> engine.services edge (doc.go), GR#3 single source.

## 2. Acceptance criteria

Observable surfaces: **View** = `f2.finance` extended with a `sankey` sub-view (additive; do not break `balanceSheet` consumers); **Ledger** = `LinesByCategory` for `benefits.*`; **Chrome** = `chrome.topbar` unchanged unless noted.

- **AC-1 (the overlap verdict, written first).** Given the developer has read `internal/engine/fiscal` and `internal/engine/finance`, then they produce a short verdict file `docs/planning/acceptance/wire-engine.fiscal-verdict.md` (the only extra doc this pack permits, authored by the developer) with a table: each fiscal export -> "merges into f2.finance view" / "wires as module hook" / "already covered by finance, drop" / "parked", each row citing the finance function it was compared against. Every row is mechanically checkable: a "drop" or "covered" row must name a finance function that returns the same quantity, and a test proves equality on a fixed ledger. The lead approves the verdict before AC-2 onward starts. *Mutation (for the equality tests):* alter the finance comparison function's sign; the equality test fails.
- **AC-2 (Sankey reachable and ledger-true).** Given a composed city after 6 monthly ticks, when the `f2.finance` view is subscribed, then it carries a `sankey` sub-view whose `MoneyIn`, `MoneyOut` and `BudgetBalance` equal figures recomputed independently from `finance.Lines`/`LinesByCategory` for the same month, node by node, and every Provisional producer node is flagged Provisional with amount 0. *Mutation:* make `MoneyIn` add a constant; the independent recompute disagrees.
- **AC-3 (drill-through reaches ledger lines).** Given the tax node, when `DrillThrough` is requested through the view/command seam, then the returned entries are exactly the ledger entries under the node's categories (count and sum match). *Mutation:* return an empty slice for a non-provisional node.
- **AC-4 (benefit postings are driven and conserved).** Given a downturn scenario (the same Scenario D defined in `wire-engine.social.md`; reuse its fixture), when unemployment rises, then a monthly hook calls `PostUnemploymentSupport` (amount from data x unemployed citizens, placeholder) so that `benefits.unemployment` lines appear as balanced treasury -> households transfers, `TotalMoneyInCirculation` is unchanged, `FindConservationViolations()` is empty, and the line total in the shock months exceeds the pre-shock months. *Mutation:* post to `AcctExternal`; conservation test fails. *Mutation:* remove the hook; no lines.
- **AC-5 (no double count).** Given fiscal benefits and social support both wired, then no citizen-month appears in both `benefits.unemployment` and the social category (see social AC-6). *Mutation:* make the social bridge also post unemployment.
- **AC-6 (municipality has a consumer).** Given planning funding lowered from full to zero, when a build-permit/cost path is exercised, then at least ONE of {permit processing time, build cost error rate} observably changes in the build flow (the dev names which; suggested: build cost error applied on `BuildAPI` cost at submit time, visible in the finance line for the construction posting). Directional. *Mutation:* ignore `BuildCostErrorRate` in the build cost; the A/B difference vanishes. If the dev finds `engine.build` forbids this without a new registered edge (`engine.build` -> `engine.fiscal` or compose-level multiplication), they register the edge in the master plan (GR#25) first, or the AC is formally deferred with Aaron's say-so (never silently).
- **AC-7 (civil-service gross vs net is real data).** Given the services wage bill supplied by the composition root (sum of `ServicesAPI.GrossWageCost` across registered services, as doc.go prescribes), when the view is read, then gross > net by the income-tax clawback at the composition's tax rate, and with services removed both are 0. *Mutation:* feed a literal wage bill; changing the real services roster does not move the view.
- **AC-8 (childcare net line).** Given childcare places set from a stated source, then the net line equals subsidy cost minus the self-funding return per data, shown as a net line in the view. If no places source exists in the composed city, the line is present and zero with a Provisional flag, never invented. *Mutation:* hard-code places.
- **AC-9 (save, determinism).** Given a loaded save, `StateDigest()` equal at the load point; same-seed runs byte-identical; fiscal state that is NOT derivable from the ledger (planning funding level, childcare places, civil-service bill) is in a save participant. *Mutation:* omit the participant; planning funding resets on load.
- **AC-10 (inputs fail closed).** Given non-finite or out-of-range planning funding, or a negative wage bill, then the command is refused with a registry error and state unchanged. *Mutation:* accept NaN.
