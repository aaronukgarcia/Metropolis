## 1. Context and goal

- **BOW:** MOD-053 (done, P2, `engine.social`, `internal/engine/social/`, 11 files, MOD-053 closed 2026-08-17 with Destructive-MOD053-r7 ACCEPT). A new wiring BOW item must be filed by the lead (this lane does not write BOW). Blocks: FEAT-138 (faith, open). Pairs with `wire-engine.fiscal.md` (same wave, see the benefit-ownership split in AC-6 and Q2 there).
- **Why:** social is "the floor of every downturn" (§40; A5 Slow-Fuse): caseload from deprivation, long-term unemployment, family stress, addiction; funded net makes declines shallower; underfunding bites a decade later. It has zero non-test importers, so in the running game nothing is ever caught by the net. `social.New(cfg Config, seed uint64, correlationID string) (*SocialAPI, error)` exists; it needs `SetCitizens`, `SetServices`, `SetFamilyStressSource`, `SetProjections`, then `RegisterServices()` and `RegisterProjectionProvider()`, and a monthly `AdvanceMonth(month, DriverInputs)`.
- **Northstar stage:** Baseline One to dogfood: "citizens consume, money moves" must include the downside path; a city with a downturn and no net is half a game.
- **Facts verified in code (2026-10-30):**
  1. **Social has NO finance edge.** `grep finance\.` in `internal/engine/social` non-test code returns nothing. Its funding goes through `engine.services` (`FundingCommand` -> `ServicesAPI.SetFunding`, `social/funding.go`) and Slow-Fuse decisions through `engine.projections`. So by itself it cannot produce a ledger transfer; the money surface must be built in the composition root (a bridge), and that needs a code.json edge (GR#25, below).
  2. **`engine.projections` is not wired in compose** (only unwired packages import it). `SetProjections` needs a real `ProjectionsAPI`; this is the same prerequisite as `wire-engine.policies.md` AC/dev direction. Whichever pack lands first constructs it; the other must reuse, not duplicate. Coordinate through the lead.
  3. **Driver inputs exist in compose or are derivable:** `DriverInputs{Deprivation, UnemploymentMonths, CrowdingStress, FinancialStress, NightlifeDensity}`. `engine.wellbeing` IS wired (`compose_wellbeing.go`, `wellbeingHook`) and supplies CrowdingStress/FinancialStress (social consumes, never recomputes them: its AC-3). Citizen employment is tracked in compose (`bug529_employment_test.go`). Deprivation and NightlifeDensity have no compose source today: placeholders need a documented source or a data constant (Q1).
  4. **Original services opex does not obviously reach the ledger in compose.** Only deathservices calls `finance.SettleOpex` (`compose.go` ~l.3499-3582). The developer must verify whether funding-level changes on social services post anywhere; if not, this is a defect in the money path this pack must close for social lines (AC-5).
  5. `protocol.KindSetFunding` already exists and maps to `ServicesAPI.SetFunding` in `handleGameplay`; social categories register as services via `RegisterServices()`, so the existing command may already be the funding lever once registered. Confirm before adding a new Kind.
- Prior rulings (look up, do not restate): A5 Slow-Fuse (`social/funding.go` `slowFuseThresholdYears`), FEAT-231 doctrine "one money" (`onemoney_guard_test.go` in compose is the existing mechanical guard), MOD-053 comments 2026-08-17 on bounded inputs (`num.SanitizeEventID` 64 chars, 1000-event ring).

## 2. Acceptance criteria

Observable surfaces: **Ledger** = `FinanceAPI.LinesByCategory` / `AccountBalance` and the `f2.finance` view; **View** = a new protocol view for caseload (suggested `f4.services` extension or a new `f4.social` sub-view; lead decides); **Chrome** = `chrome.topbar`.

The scripted downturn used by AC-2..AC-6 ("Scenario D"): same seed, two compositions A (social funding 1.0) and B (social funding at the data-declared minimum), a populated start city, 12 steady months, then from month 12 a **job-shock**: employment-generating workplaces are removed or their wage payments zeroed through the existing compose test harness (developer picks the existing helper; the `bug529_employment_test.go` fixtures are the starting point) so that long-term unemployment months rise; run to month 36. Scenario D is a test fixture, not a game feature.

- **AC-1 (reachability).** Given `compose.Wire` with default Deps, then `RegistrationOrder()` includes a social monthly hook, the social service categories are registered with `engine.services`, and a Subscribe to the caseload view returns five categories (family support, homelessness, disability and carers, fostering, addiction) with open counts. *Mutation:* remove the `registrationOrder` row; both the order test and the view test fail.
- **AC-2 (steady state is quiet).** Given the steady 12 months before the shock, then caseload is non-zero but bounded and identical across two same-seed runs (determinism) and the people-conservation invariant holds every tick. *Mutation:* seed from the wall clock or `math/rand`; the equality fails.
- **AC-3 (downturn raises caseload).** Given Scenario D, when the job-shock lands, then homelessness and family-support open caseload in A at month 36 is strictly greater than at month 12 (directional only). *Mutation:* feed `UnemploymentMonths: 0` constantly; caseload does not rise, test fails. This also proves the driver is read from real citizen state rather than a literal.
- **AC-4 (a funded net makes the decline shallower).** Given Scenario D for A and B, then peak open caseload in A is lower than in B by the end of the run, OR rough sleeping in A is lower (the mechanism: prevention, hostels, housing first). Directional, not a number. *Mutation:* make `FundingLevel` ignore funding; A equals B.
- **AC-5 (observable transfer in the one-money ledger).** Given Scenario D in A, when caseload exists, then each month a balanced treasury-to-households support transfer line appears under a social ledger category (suggest `social.support`; naming through the same open `finance.Category` string the fiscal package uses), sized from social's own `Accounting` output (cost per open case x funding level, placeholder data), `TotalMoneyInCirculation` is unchanged by the transfer (internal transfer, money delta zero) and `FindConservationViolations()` is empty. During the shock months the line's total is greater than the pre-shock months' line total. *Mutation:* post to `AcctExternal` instead of households (money leaks out; the conservation check fails), and separately delete the posting (no line; AC fails).
- **AC-6 (no double count with fiscal benefits).** Given both social and fiscal are wired (or fiscal's `PostUnemploymentSupport` is called anywhere), then a given citizen-month of unemployment support is posted by exactly one owner. Social owns caseload service support (the five categories); fiscal owns unemployment and housing benefit (`benefits.unemployment`, `benefits.housing`). Test: sum of both category lines for a fixed scenario equals the sum of two independently computed amounts, with no category posted twice per month. *Mutation:* call both for the same citizens; the assertion catches the duplicate.
- **AC-7 (underfunding is a priced, visible decision).** Given an attempt to cut a Slow-Fuse social funding level (principal effect beyond 5 game-years) over the command seam without a projected consequence, then the command is refused with the registry error and no funding change; with a rendered consequence series it is accepted and the consequence appears in the view. *Mutation:* bypass the Slow-Fuse gate; the unprojected cut succeeds.
- **AC-8 (crisis injection is bounded).** Given `InjectCrisis` with an event ID over 64 characters or empty, then it is refused with registry codes MET-F805 / MET-F804 and nothing is appended; the ring never exceeds 1000 non-ledger events over a long run. *Mutation:* raise the ring cap in config; the bound test fails. (Regression coverage already exists at package level; the new test proves it still holds through the composed command path if one is exposed, otherwise record N/A with reason.)
- **AC-9 (save/load round-trips social state).** Given Scenario D at month 24, when saved and loaded and `StateDigest()` compared at the load point, then equal, including open cases and funding levels. *Mutation:* leave social out of the save participant list; digests differ.
