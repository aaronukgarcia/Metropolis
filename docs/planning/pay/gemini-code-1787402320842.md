# System Prompt: Floating Debt & LIBOR Mechanics

## 1. Core Borrowing Directives
The player acts as the sovereign municipality. They cannot print money, but they can issue municipal bonds to borrow from the global market. 
* All debt is issued at a variable, floating interest rate called `LIBOR`.
* Interest ($I$) is calculated and deducted on a standard tick interval (e.g., monthly or annually).
* $I = D \times r_{libor}$ where $D$ is total outstanding principal.

## 2. Dynamic Debt Ceiling (The Default Trigger)
Do NOT implement a static borrowing limit. The player's ability to borrow is dynamically capped by their Net Operating Income (NOI).

* **NOI Calculation:** $NOI = R_{tax} - E_{civic}$ 
  *(Where $R_{tax}$ is total tax revenue and $E_{civic}$ is total civic wages + maintenance).*
* **Borrowing Capacity:** $C_{debt} = NOI / r_{libor}$
* **The Default State:** If the player's required interest payment ($I$) exceeds their $NOI$ for 3 consecutive ticks, the city enters **Municipal Default**. During Default, the player cannot build new structures, and civic buildings operate at 50% capacity until debt is paid down or NOI is increased.

## 3. The Rate Fluctuation Engine
The `LIBOR` rate is not static. It recalculates at the start of every in-game year based on the player's `Generosity` slider ($G$) and market volatility.

* **Base Rate:** 2.0%
* **Generosity Premium:** Add 1.0% to the rate for every point of $G$ above 0. (e.g., $G = +3$ adds 3.0%). If $G$ is negative, subtract 0.5% per point (deflationary).
* **Market Shock:** Apply a randomized `Shock Value` between -1.0% and +2.0% annually to simulate external macro conditions.
* **Absolute Floor:** $r_{libor}$ can never drop below 0.5%.

## 4. UI Requirements
* **The Debt Dashboard:** Always display current Outstanding Debt alongside the Dynamic Debt Ceiling. 
* **Rate Warning:** When $r_{libor}$ shifts, prominently display the new rate. If the rate hike causes the player's Interest Payments to exceed 80% of their NOI, trigger a flashing "Over-leveraged Warning" UI element.
