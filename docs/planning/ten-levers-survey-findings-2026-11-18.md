## Surprises
1. Services is not "wire a term": the term exists and is permanently 100 because nothing pushes demand; a clinic would add capacity against zero demand and move nothing. The lever needs the demand push. Health care shares that bridge and is nearly free after it, except for the missing HealthcareAccess.
2. Policy is the cheapest lever after housing: EnactPolicy is handled, saved, digested; nationalMinimumWage (citywide) already moves migration via affordability. It needs only a terminal screen. Pulling it earlier than 5th is nearly free; parkAccess is blocked by district scope.
3. Parks (and shops via the same bridge) are cheap: the entertainment-zone -> leisure-venue bridge exists.
4. Jobs is bigger than it looks: zone jobCapacity is unread, firms are never founded except the builders' merchant, the 0.20-weight term is dead.
5. Transport, congestion, tourism are L: roads/rail/airport/tourism are not composed. The placement catalogue (BuildingID through KindBuild, real catalogue published to F3) is the shared critical path for services, health care, parks, transport and tourism - one lane there unlocks all; doing it first changes the order.
6. JunctionThroughput is a 1,000,000 constant so the cap transport/congestion would constrain never binds.
7. "Start empty" breaks the founder-id range check and assumptions in ~112 Wire-calling test files; budget a fixture lane.
8. District-scoped policies need a district command that does not exist.
